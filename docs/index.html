02 · Five layers

Five layers.
One perfect bite.

A toasted base, cold crisp lettuce, ripe tomato, a thin patty smashed onto a smoking pan under melting cheddar, and a soft sesame brioche on top.

03 · The crust

Smash it thin.
Let it crust.

Press the beef flat on a very hot pan, then leave it alone for two minutes. More surface on the steel means more browning: crisp, lacy edges and deep flavour.

04 · The melt

Flip. Cheddar on.
Under a minute to melt.

Cheese goes on the moment the patty is flipped, then 45 to 60 seconds more on the heat. It slumps into every crisp edge while the beef finishes cooking.
