Chicken Parmigiana with Spaghetti

Inspired by techniques from America's Test Kitchen and Serious Eats.

Chicken parmigiana · Classic · Italian-American

Crumbed chicken cutlets are fried until golden, topped with tomato sauce and melted mozzarella, then served over spaghetti.

Prep25 min
Cook40 min
Total1 h 5 min
DifficultyMedium
Per serving883 kcal
Free
Servings4Recipe makes 4

Ingredients

Metric · 4 servings

Chicken

  • 2 large boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 300 g each), halved horizontally into 4 cutlets
  • 1.5 g fine sea salt or table salt about ¼ tsp
  • ¼ tsp ground black pepper

Coating

  • 50 g plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 80 g panko or dried breadcrumbs
  • 20 g finely grated parmesan
  • 400 ml neutral oil for shallow frying about 1 cm deep

Tomato sauce

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 800 g tinned crushed or chopped tomatoes, no added salt (2 tins)
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • 1.5 g fine sea salt or table salt about ¼ tsp; the tomatoes have no added salt; taste and adjust
  • 8 g basil leaves

Topping

  • 125 g mozzarella, torn or sliced
  • 20 g grated parmesan

Spaghetti

  • 280 g dried spaghetti
  • 7.5 g fine sea salt or table salt, for the pasta water (7.5 g in 1.5 litres, 0.5%) about 1¼ tsp

Method

  1. 1

    Simmer the tomato sauce. Warm the olive oil and garlic in a saucepan over medium-low heat for 1-2 minutes until pale gold, then add the no-added-salt tomatoes, oregano, the ¼ tsp salt and a few basil stems. Simmer for 15-20 minutes until thickened, then taste and adjust the seasoning.

  2. 2

    Flatten and season the chicken. Pound the cutlets between cling film to an even 6 mm thickness, then season with the salt and pepper.

  3. 3

    Coat the cutlets. Dip each cutlet in flour, then beaten egg, then the panko mixed with parmesan, pressing the crumb on firmly so it holds.

  4. 4

    Fry until golden. Heat the oil to about 1 cm deep in the frying pan over medium-high until it reaches around 175°C, hot enough that a crumb sizzles instantly. Fry the cutlets 2 at a time for about 2 minutes per side until deep golden and the centre reaches 74°C, then drain on the rack.

  5. 5

    Grill with sauce and cheese. Heat the grill (broiler) to high. Set the cutlets on the rack-lined tray, spoon 2 tablespoons of sauce onto the centre of each, leaving the crisp edges bare, then top with the mozzarella and parmesan. Grill for 3-4 minutes until the cheese is melted, bubbling and browned in spots.

  6. 6

    Cook the pasta and serve. While the cutlets grill, cook the spaghetti in 1.5 litres of boiling water salted with 7.5 g (1¼ tsp) salt - a light 0.5% because the sauce and cheese are already salty - for 1 minute less than the packet suggests, then drain and toss with most of the remaining sauce. Serve the cutlets on the spaghetti, scattered with basil.

Tips

  • Leave the outer edges of each cutlet free of sauce and cheese before grilling so they stay crisp rather than turning soggy.
  • Common mistake: drowning the fried cutlets in sauce. A spoonful in the centre is enough; serve the rest alongside with the pasta.
  • Check the oil temperature before frying - too cool and the crumb turns greasy rather than crisp.
  • Pound the chicken to an even thickness so it cooks through at the same rate as the crust browns.
  • Undercook the pasta slightly before draining, since it will keep softening slightly while you plate up.
  • Frying safety: use a heavy, deep pot no more than half full of oil, with a clip-on thermometer, and dry the food before it goes in. Never leave hot oil unattended. If the oil catches fire, turn off the heat and cover the pot with a metal lid or use a fire extinguisher; never use water.

Storage

Store sauce and cooked cutlets separately in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat cutlets in the oven or under the grill until piping hot (74°C) to keep the crumb crisp; a microwave will make it soggy. Cool leftovers in a shallow container and refrigerate within 2 hours (within 1 hour on a day above 32°C).

Equipment

  • large frying pan (28-30 cm)
  • medium saucepan
  • large pot for pasta
  • meat mallet or rolling pin
  • 3 shallow dishes
  • rimmed baking tray with a rack
  • instant-read thermometer
