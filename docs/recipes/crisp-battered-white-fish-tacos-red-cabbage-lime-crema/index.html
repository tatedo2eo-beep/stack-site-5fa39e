Crisp Battered White Fish Tacos with Red Cabbage and Lime Crema

Inspired by techniques from Serious Eats, NYT Cooking and America's Test Kitchen.

Taco · Fish · Mexican

Crunchy sparkling-water-battered white fish in warm corn tortillas with tangy lime crema and crisp red cabbage.

Prep30 min
Cook30 min
Total1 h
DifficultyMedium
