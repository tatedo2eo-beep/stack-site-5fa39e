High-Protein Greek Yoghurt Berry Bowl

Thick, high-protein Greek yoghurt gets swirled with a quick no-sugar berry compote and topped with crunchy toasted almonds and chia seeds. It's a simple, naturally sweet bowl that comes together in minutes.

Ingredients

1 servings
  • 300 gfat-free Greek yoghurt
  • 150 gmixed berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries), fresh or frozen and thawed
  • 10 gflaked almonds
  • 1 tspchia seeds
  • 1 pinchground cinnamon
  • 1 pinchfinely grated lemon zest optional

Equipment

  • small frying pan (for toasting almonds)
  • serving bowl
  • fork

Allergens (EU 14)

  • Milk
  • Tree nuts

Always check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Toast almonds

    Toast the flaked almonds in a dry small pan over medium-low heat for 2-3 minutes, shaking the pan often, until golden and fragrant, then tip them straight out onto a plate. They can turn from golden to burnt within seconds and will keep cooking in the pan's residual heat, so getting them out promptly matters.

  2. Prepare the berries

    If using frozen berries, thaw them in a bowl in the fridge overnight, or for about 30 minutes at room temperature, keeping any juice that collects, as it's naturally sweet and worth stirring through. Hull and halve any large strawberries, then lightly crush about a third of the berries with a fork to make a quick, no-sugar compote that will swirl nicely through the yoghurt.

  3. Assemble the bowl

    Stir the cinnamon and lemon zest into the yoghurt, as both make it taste noticeably sweeter and more rounded without any added sugar. Spoon the yoghurt into a bowl, swirl through the crushed berries and their juices, then finish with the remaining berries, toasted almonds and chia seeds on top.

Tips

  • You won't need any honey or sugar here - ripe or thawed berries plus a pinch of cinnamon give plenty of sweetness on their own.
  • Fat-free Greek yoghurt is thick and high in protein; regular (non-Greek) yoghurt has roughly half the protein, so it won't give quite the same result.
  • Add the almonds just before you eat, rather than stirring them straight in, so they keep their crunch instead of going soft in the yoghurt.
  • Short on berries? Swap in other fruit like cherries or kiwi for a similar effect.

Storage

Best assembled just before eating. Yoghurt mixed with the crushed berries can be covered and kept in the fridge for up to 1 day; add the almonds and chia only when you're ready to serve.
