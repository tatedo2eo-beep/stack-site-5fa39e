Baked Beef Empanadas with Olives and Raisins

Inspired by techniques from America's Test Kitchen, NYT Cooking and Serious Eats.

Empanadas · Classic · Argentinian

Spiced beef mince bound with a bread paste is cooked with onion, cumin and cloves, finished with raisins, olives and vinegar and chilled, then folded into masa pastry and baked on hot oiled trays until crisp.

Ingredients

Metric · 6 servings

Dough

  • 425 g plain flour, plus extra for rolling
  • 140 g masa harina
  • 12 g sugar about 1 tbsp
  • 6 g fine sea salt or table salt about 1 tsp
  • 170 g unsalted butter, cubed and chilled
  • 240 ml ice-cold water

Filling

  • 1 slice white sandwich bread, torn
  • 150 ml unsalted or low-sodium chicken stock 2 tbsp for the bread paste, the rest for simmering
  • 450 g beef mince (15% fat), from a butcher
  • 3 g fine sea salt or table salt about ½ tsp
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped about 300 g
  • 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 pinch ground cloves
  • 10 g fresh coriander, chopped
  • 50 g raisins, chopped
  • 35 g pitted green olives, chopped
  • 4 tsp cider vinegar

To bake

  • 5 tbsp olive oil, for baking

Method

  1. 1

    Make the paste. Blend the bread with a little stock, then pulse in the beef and seasoning.

  2. 2

    Cook the filling. Brown the onions, add spices and beef and cook to 71 °C, then simmer with stock until moist.

  3. 3

    Chill. Stir in coriander, raisins, olives and vinegar and chill about 1 hour.

  4. 4

    Make the dough. Process the flours, sugar, salt and butter, then mix in ice water; divide into 12 and chill.

  5. 5

    Shape. Roll into 15 cm rounds, fill, fold, seal and crimp with a fork.

  6. 6

    Bake. Bake on preheated oiled trays at 220 °C for 25-30 minutes until browned.

Tips

  • Cold filling keeps the pastry from going soft before it bakes.
  • Preheating the oiled trays gives a crisp, fried-style base.
  • Shaped empanadas freeze well; bake from frozen with 5 extra minutes.

Storage

Refrigerate within 2 hours for up to 3 days. Reheat at 190 °C for 10-15 minutes until piping hot (74 °C in the centre).

Equipment

  • food processor
  • large non-stick frying pan
  • wooden spoon
  • instant-read thermometer
  • large bowl
  • rolling pin
  • fork
  • oven
  • 2 large baking trays
  • pastry brush
  • wire rack
