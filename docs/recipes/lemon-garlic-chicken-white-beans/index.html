Lemon-garlic chicken with white beans and spinach

Seared chicken breast sliced over a one-pan stew of creamy white beans, blistered cherry tomatoes and wilted spinach, brightened with lemon and smoked paprika. Big on protein and fibre, light on saturated fat, on the table in half an hour.

Ingredients

4 servings

Chicken

  • 600 gskinless chicken breast fillets about 3 large
  • 1 tspsmoked paprika
  • 2 gfine salt about ⅓ tsp
  • 1 tbspolive oil

Beans and greens

  • 4 clovesgarlic, thinly sliced
  • 250 gcherry tomatoes
  • 480 gcooked cannellini beans, drained and rinsed 2 tins
  • 150 mllow-salt chicken stock check the label if you need it gluten-free
  • 200 gbaby spinach
  • 1lemon zest and 2 tbsp juice
  • to tasteblack pepper

Equipment

  • Large frying pan or cast-iron skillet with a lid
  • Chopping board and knife
  • Colander
  • Fine grater or zester

Allergens (EU 14)

  • Celery

Always check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Even out the chicken

    Lay the chicken breasts between two sheets of baking paper and flatten the thick ends with a rolling pin so each fillet is an even 2 cm thick. Rub all over with the paprika, salt and a good grind of pepper.

  2. Sear the chicken

    Heat the oil in the pan over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 5 to 6 minutes per side without moving it, until golden and cooked through (75 °C in the thickest part, or the juices run clear). Move to a board to rest.

  3. Blister the tomatoes

    Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic and tomatoes to the same pan and cook for 3 minutes, stirring now and then, until the tomatoes start to split and the garlic is pale gold. Don't let the garlic brown.

  4. Simmer the beans

    Tip in the beans and stock, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom. Simmer for 4 minutes, lightly crushing a few beans with the back of a spoon so the liquid turns creamy.

  5. Wilt the spinach

    Add the spinach a handful at a time, stirring until it collapses, then stir in the lemon zest and juice. Taste and add more pepper or a squeeze of lemon if it needs it.

  6. Slice and serve

    Slice the rested chicken, pour any juices from the board back into the pan and lay the slices on top of the beans. Serve straight from the pan.

Tips

  • Flattening the chicken to an even thickness is what keeps it juicy: thin ends no longer overcook while the thick end catches up.
  • Rinsing tinned beans removes a good part of the salt from the tin liquid.
  • Swap the spinach for chopped kale or chard; give them 2 to 3 minutes longer.

Storage

Cool quickly and keep covered in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a splash of water until piping hot. The beans and chicken also freeze well for up to 2 months; the spinach goes softer but is fine.
