Thai Green Coconut Curry with Chicken, Aubergine and Basil

Inspired by techniques from NYT Cooking and Serious Eats.

Green curry · Chicken · Thai

A vibrant, fragrant green curry where chicken thighs and tender aubergine simmer in a properly cracked coconut paste, finished with wilted Thai basil and lime leaf.

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Total35 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

Ingredient names · quantities with Premium

  • full-fat coconut milk (1 can), divided
  • water
  • makrut lime leaves, central rib removed
  • virgin coconut oil
  • store-bought Thai green curry paste
  • palm sugar or light brown sugar
  • fish sauce
  • boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 5 cm pieces
  • Thai round aubergines, quartered and kept in a bowl of water
  • Thai basil leaves
