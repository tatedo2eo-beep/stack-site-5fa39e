Nigerian Chicken Pepper Soup with Red Pepper Broth

Inspired by techniques from NYT Cooking.

Pepper soup · Chicken · Nigerian

A lighter, faster pepper soup: chicken legs cooked in a blended red pepper, onion, ginger and Scotch bonnet base with warm pepper-soup spices, finished with basil.
