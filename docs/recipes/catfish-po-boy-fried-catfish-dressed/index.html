Po’ Boy with Fried Catfish (Dressed)

Inspired by techniques from Serious Eats.

Po' boy · Classic · American (New Orleans)

Catfish soaked in buttermilk and egg, coated in cornmeal, cornflour and salt-free Creole spices, chilled, then deep-fried at 185 °C until crisp. Served on toasted French bread with mayonnaise, tomato, pickles and lettuce.

Ingredients

Metric · 4 servings

Catfish

  • 4 catfish fillets, halved along the natural seam about 140 g each
  • 120 ml buttermilk
  • 1 large egg
  • 150 g fine cornmeal
  • 30 g cornflour (cornstarch)

Salt-free Creole seasoning

  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper

Catfish

  • 1.5 l neutral frying oil (sunflower, rapeseed or vegetable) for 4-5 cm depth

To dress

  • 4 sections of soft French bread or po’ boy rolls, about 20 cm long, split and lightly toasted about 90 g each
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 medium tomatoes, thinly sliced
  • 12 dill pickle slices
  • 120 g iceberg lettuce, finely shredded
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges

Method

  1. 1

    Soak the fish. Whisk the buttermilk and egg in a shallow dish and soak the catfish for 5 min. The tangy liquid helps the coating cling.

  2. 2

    Coat and chill. Mix the cornmeal, cornflour and Creole seasoning. Lift each piece from the buttermilk, let it drip, press it into the coating on all sides and set it on a rack. Chill for 15 min so the crust firms and stays on in the oil.

  3. 3

    Fry. Heat oil 5 cm deep to 185 °C. Fry the fish in two batches for 4-5 min, turning once, until deep golden and the thickest part reaches 63 °C. Drain on the paper-lined rack.

  4. 4

    Dress and serve. Spread the toasted bread with mayonnaise, lay tomato and pickles on the top halves and lettuce on the bottoms, then pile on the fish. Press together and serve at once with lemon wedges.

Cat tip

Fry in small batches so the oil stays hot and the crust stays crisp.

More from the cat

  • Add a dash of hot sauce to the mayonnaise for a kick.
  • Cod or another firm white fish works if you can't find catfish.
  • Frying safety: use a heavy, deep pot no more than half full of oil, with a clip-on thermometer, and dry the food before it goes in. Never leave hot oil unattended. If the oil catches fire, turn off the heat and cover the pot with a metal lid or use a fire extinguisher; never use water.

Storage

Best eaten immediately. The fried fish can be re-crisped in a 200 °C oven for 8-10 min until 63 °C, then assembled fresh. Cool leftovers in a shallow container and refrigerate within 2 hours (within 1 hour on a day above 32°C).

Equipment

  • shallow dish
  • 2 wire racks set over trays
  • kitchen paper
  • large Dutch oven
  • deep-fry thermometer
  • spider or slotted spoon
