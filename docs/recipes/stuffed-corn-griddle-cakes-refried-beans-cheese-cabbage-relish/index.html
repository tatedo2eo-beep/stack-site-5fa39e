Stuffed Corn Griddle Cakes with Refried Beans, Cheese and Cabbage Relish

Inspired by techniques from Serious Eats, America's Test Kitchen and NYT Cooking.

Pupusa · Bean-cheese · Salvadoran

These corn masa cakes hide a creamy mashed pinto bean and melted cheese filling, griddled until crisp and spotted brown. They're served warm with a quick tangy cabbage relish and a simple tomato salsa for balance.
