Vegetable Fried Rice with Red Pepper, Peas and Sweetcorn

Inspired by techniques from America's Test Kitchen and Serious Eats.

Fried rice · Vegetable · Chinese

This follows the same egg fried rice method as our classic recipe, with the egg cooked first and set aside, then carrot, red pepper, garlic, ginger, peas and sweetcorn stir-fried before the rice goes in and the egg returns at the end.

Prep15 min
Cook15 min
Total30 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

Ingredient names · quantities with Premium

  • cold cooked long-grain or jasmine rice (from about 200 g raw rice, cooked the day before)
  • large eggs
  • fine sea salt or table salt
  • neutral oil
  • spring onions, whites and greens sliced separately
  • soy sauce (alcohol-free)
  • ground white pepper
  • carrot, cut into 5 mm dice
  • red pepper, deseeded and cut into 1 cm dice
  • garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • fresh ginger, finely grated
  • frozen peas
  • drained tinned sweetcorn
  • neutral oil
