Curried Chicken Salad with Mango Chutney, Currants and Almonds

Inspired by techniques from America's Test Kitchen, NYT Cooking and Serious Eats.

Chicken salad · Curry · British

This spiced take follows the same gentle poaching method as our classic chicken salad, but the dressing is built on bloomed curry powder, mango chutney, lime and yoghurt, with currants, toasted almonds and coriander stirred through.

Prep20 min
Cook30 min

DifficultyEasy

  • spring onions, thinly sliced
  • dried currants or golden raisins
  • flaked almonds, toasted
  • coriander leaves, chopped
