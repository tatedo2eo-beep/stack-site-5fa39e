Sticky Toffee Pudding with Date Sponge and Toffee Sauce

Inspired by techniques from Serious Eats.

Sticky toffee pudding · Classic · British

A moist date sponge soaked in a buttery toffee sauce, served warm with extra sauce and a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Prep30 min
Cook1 h 10 min
Total1 h 40 min
DifficultyMedium
Per serving724 kcal
FreeVegetarian
Servings15Recipe makes 15

Ingredients

Metric · 15 servings

Sponge

  • 450 g pitted Medjool dates
  • 480 ml water
  • 2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 1.5 g fine sea salt or table salt about ¼ tsp
  • 450 g plain flour, plus extra for the tin
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 140 g unsalted butter, at room temperature, plus extra for the tin
  • 450 g sugar
  • 5 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 2 tsp vanilla

Toffee sauce

  • 225 g unsalted butter
  • 225 g dark brown sugar
  • 1.5 g fine sea salt or table salt about ¼ tsp
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • 240 ml double cream

To serve

  • 15 scoops vanilla ice cream, to serve

Method

  1. 1

    Simmer. Cook 450 g dates in 480 ml water for 5 min until soft, then off the heat stir in 2 tsp bicarbonate of soda and ¼ tsp salt, cool 10 min and purée smooth.

  2. 2

    Mix. Whisk 450 g flour and 2 tsp baking powder together.

  3. 3

    Cream. Beat 140 g butter and 450 g sugar until pale and fluffy, 3-4 min, then beat in 5 eggs one at a time and 2 tsp vanilla.

  4. 4

    Combine. On low speed add the flour in three additions, alternating with the date purée, mixing just until combined.

  5. 5

    Bake. Spread the batter in a buttered and floured tin and bake at 175 °C until risen and a skewer comes out clean, 50-60 min.

  6. 6

    Sauce. Melt 225 g butter, 225 g dark brown sugar and ¼ tsp salt until bubbling, then whisk in 240 ml cream and ½ tsp vanilla and simmer 2-3 min.

  7. 7

    Soak. Poke the warm cake all over with a skewer and pour over about a third of the sauce, letting it soak in for 10 min.

  8. 8

    Serve. Cut into squares and serve warm with more sauce and a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Cat tip

Let the bicarbonate of soda foam into the hot date mixture fully before puréeing for a soft, moist crumb.

More from the cat

  • Add the flour and date purée in alternating additions on low speed to avoid overworking the batter.
  • Poke the warm sponge all over before pouring over the sauce so it soaks right through.
  • Don't taste the raw dough or batter: raw flour and raw egg aren't safe to eat until baked.

Storage

Best served warm the day it's made; keep any leftovers covered and reheat gently with a little extra sauce.

Equipment

  • 33 x 23 cm baking tin
  • medium saucepan
  • stick blender or food processor
  • medium bowl
  • stand mixer with paddle
  • spatula
  • skewer
  • wire rack
