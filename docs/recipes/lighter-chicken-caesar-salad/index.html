Lighter Chicken Caesar Salad

A lighter take on the classic chicken Caesar, swapping most of the oil-heavy dressing for Greek yoghurt while keeping the garlicky, anchovy-rich flavour and crunchy croutons.

Ingredients

4 servings

Chicken

  • 600 gskinless chicken breast fillets
  • 2 tspolive oil
  • 1 pinchfine salt the dressing brings plenty of salty flavour
  • ½ tspground black pepper

Croutons

  • 100 gsourdough or crusty white bread, torn into 2 cm pieces
  • 2 tspolive oil
  • ¼ tspgarlic powder

Dressing

  • 150 gfat-free Greek yoghurt
  • 25 gmayonnaise
  • 25 gParmesan-style hard cheese, very finely grated
  • 3anchovy fillets in oil, drained and finely chopped about 12 g
  • 1small garlic clove, finely grated
  • 2 tbsplemon juice
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • ½ tspground black pepper
  • 1 tbspwater, to loosen

Salad

  • 500 gromaine (cos) lettuce about 2 large heads, leaves separated, washed and dried
  • 10 gParmesan-style hard cheese, shaved, to finish

Equipment

  • large frying pan or griddle pan (28 cm)
  • baking tray
  • meat mallet or rolling pin and baking paper
  • fine grater
  • instant-read thermometer
  • large salad bowl

Allergens (EU 14)

  • Cereals containing gluten
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Milk
  • Mustard

Always check the labels of the products you use.

Method

  1. Bake the croutons

    Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Toss the torn bread with the oil and garlic powder on a baking tray, then bake for 8-10 minutes, shaking the tray once, until golden and crisp all the way through - they'll crisp up further as they cool. Tearing the bread into rough pieces rather than cutting neat cubes gives more ragged edges to catch oil and turn crunchy.

  2. Prepare the chicken

    Place the chicken fillets between sheets of baking paper and pound the thicker end with a mallet or rolling pin until each is an even 2 cm thick. Making the thickness consistent means the whole fillet cooks at the same rate, so you won't end up with dry edges and an underdone centre. Rub both sides with the oil, salt and pepper.

  3. Cook the chicken

    Cook the fillets in a frying or griddle pan over medium-high heat for 5-6 minutes per side without moving them, until golden with good grill marks and the thickest part reaches 74°C. Rest the chicken on a board for 5 minutes before slicing. Resting gives the juices time to settle back through the meat instead of running out onto the board the moment you cut into it.

  4. Make the dressing

    Mash the anchovies and grated garlic together to a rough paste using the flat side of a knife, which melts the anchovy into the dressing as a savoury background note rather than leaving obvious fishy bits. Whisk this paste with the yoghurt, mayonnaise, Parmesan-style cheese, lemon juice, Dijon, pepper and water until smooth and easily pourable. The mustard and small amount of mayonnaise help hold the dressing together so it doesn't separate.

  5. Toss the salad

    Make sure the romaine leaves are completely dry before you start, since any clinging water dilutes the dressing and stops it clinging to the leaves. Tear the lettuce into large pieces and toss with two-thirds of the dressing just before serving. Dressing at the last minute keeps the leaves crisp rather than wilted.

  6. Assemble and serve

    Pile the dressed lettuce onto plates or a serving bowl, then top with the sliced chicken, croutons and shaved Parmesan-style cheese. Drizzle over the remaining dressing. Serve straight away while the croutons are still crisp.

Tips

  • Dry the lettuce thoroughly before dressing it - wet leaves dilute the dressing and let it slide straight off.
  • This dressing tastes strongest right after mixing and mellows a little as it sits, so taste and adjust just before serving.
  • For an anchovy-free version, leave them out and add a small pinch of extra salt; the dressing will taste milder but still good.
  • Dress the salad at the very last minute - dressed leaves don't stay crisp for long.
  • Keep baked croutons in an airtight container rather than leaving them exposed, or they'll turn stale and lose their crunch.

Storage

Keep the components separate if making ahead: chicken in the fridge for up to 3 days, dressing for up to 3 days, and croutons in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days. Once dressed, the salad doesn't keep and should be eaten straight away; reheat chicken until piping hot (74°C) if serving it warm again.
