Anise Biscotti

Inspired by techniques from King Arthur Baking, America's Test Kitchen and NYT Cooking.

Biscotti · Anise · Italian

These anise biscotti follow the classic almond biscotti method, with lightly crushed anise seeds and lemon zest replacing the orange zest and fewer almonds for a lighter, more aniseed-forward bite.
