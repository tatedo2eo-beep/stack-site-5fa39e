Pistachio Cannoli: Crisp Shells with Ricotta-Mascarpone Pistachio Cream

Inspired by techniques from America's Test Kitchen and NYT Cooking.

Cannoli · Pistachio · Italian

Crisp homemade cannoli shells piped with a smooth ricotta and mascarpone cream full of pistachio, their ends dipped in finely chopped green pistachios.
