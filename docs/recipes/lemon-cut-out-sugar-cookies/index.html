Lemon Cut-Out Sugar Cookies

Inspired by techniques from King Arthur Baking and NYT Cooking.

Sugar/cut-out · Lemon · American

These follow the classic cut-out sugar cookie method, with lemon zest rubbed into the sugar for a fragrant dough and a lemon juice glaze in place of milk.

Prep35 min
Cook12 min
Total47 min
DifficultyMedium
