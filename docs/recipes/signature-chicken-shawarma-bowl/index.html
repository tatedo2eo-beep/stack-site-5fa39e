Signature High-Protein Chicken Shawarma Bowl

Spiced yoghurt-marinated chicken breast is roasted until charred at the edges, then sliced over fluffy basmati rice with a fresh salad and garlicky yoghurt sauce for a high-protein bowl.

Ingredients

4 servings

Chicken

  • 720 gskinless chicken breast fillets about 4 fillets
  • 60 gfat-free Greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 2 tbsplemon juice
  • 3garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 2 tspground cumin
  • 2 tspsweet paprika
  • 1 tspground coriander
  • ½ tspground turmeric
  • ¼ tspground cinnamon
  • ¼ tspcayenne pepper
  • ¾ tspfine salt

Rice

  • 160 gbasmati rice
  • 240 mlwater
  • 1 pinchfine salt

Salad

  • 200 gcucumber, diced
  • 200 gripe tomatoes, diced
  • 60 gred onion, finely sliced
  • 15 gflat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 1 tbsplemon juice

Garlic yoghurt sauce

  • 250 gfat-free Greek yoghurt
  • 1small garlic clove, finely grated
  • 1 tbsplemon juice
  • 1 pinchfine salt
  • 1 tbspwater, to loosen

Equipment

  • large bowl
  • large rimmed baking tray lined with baking paper
  • medium saucepan with a tight lid
  • sieve
  • digital probe thermometer

Allergens (EU 14)

  • Milk

Method

  1. Even out the chicken

    If any of the chicken fillets are thicker at one end, slice them horizontally into two thinner escalopes, each about 1.5-2 cm thick. Evening out the thickness means every piece cooks through at the same rate, so you won't end up with a dry, tapered end next to an undercooked thick one.

  2. Marinate the chicken

    Mix the yoghurt, oil, lemon juice, garlic, spices and salt together, then coat the chicken thoroughly. Leave at room temperature for up to 30 minutes, or cover and refrigerate for up to 24 hours. The yoghurt helps the chicken brown in the oven and keeps the lean breast meat moist, but don't go beyond 24 hours, as the acid starts to make the texture mushy.

  3. Heat the oven

    An oven heated to 230°C (210°C fan), with a shelf positioned near the top, is what you need before the chicken goes in. Spread the marinated chicken out on a lined tray, leaving space between the pieces so they roast rather than stew in their own juices.

  4. Cook the rice

    Rinse the basmati in a sieve under running water until it runs almost clear, which washes off the surface starch and helps the grains stay separate. Put it in a saucepan with the 240 ml water and a pinch of salt, bring to the boil, cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat for 12 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave the lid on for a further 10 minutes so the last of the steam finishes softening the grains, then fluff with a fork.

  5. Roast the chicken

    Fifteen to twenty minutes in the oven is enough for the edges to brown and char in spots and the thickest part to reach 74°C. For extra char, finish under a hot grill (broiler) for the final 2 minutes, watching closely so the spices don't catch.

  6. Rest and slice

    Rest the chicken for 5 minutes so the juices redistribute through the meat as it finishes cooking from residual heat. Slice it thinly across the grain, the way shawarma is traditionally cut, then toss the slices through the juices left on the tray.

  7. Salad and sauce

    Toss the cucumber, tomatoes, red onion and parsley with the lemon juice just before serving — cucumber and tomato start to weep once dressed, so mixing too early leaves a watery bowl. Stir together the yoghurt, garlic, lemon juice and salt for the sauce, loosening it with the water until it's spoonable.

  8. Assemble the bowls

    Divide the rice between bowls, then top with the sliced chicken, a generous scoop of salad and a spoonful of the garlic yoghurt sauce. Scatter over chilli flakes if you'd like extra heat.

Tips

  • Use a thermometer rather than guesswork — breast meat turns dry fast once it overshoots 74°C.
  • Slicing thicker fillets into thinner escalopes before marinating means the whole batch cooks evenly.
  • Heavy spices like paprika and cumin can scorch quickly under the grill, so watch closely in those last 2 minutes rather than walking away.
  • Many spice flavours dissolve in fat, so the olive oil in the marinade spreads them evenly over every piece instead of leaving dry patches of powder.
  • This bowl is ideal for meal prep — cook a batch of chicken and rice at the start of the week and assemble fresh each day.

Storage

Keep chicken and rice in separate airtight containers in the fridge for up to 3 days. Cool the rice quickly, within 1 hour of cooking, and reheat it only once until piping hot throughout. The yoghurt sauce keeps for 3 days, but dress the salad fresh on the day you eat it.
