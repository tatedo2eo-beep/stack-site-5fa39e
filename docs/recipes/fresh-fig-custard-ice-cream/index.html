Fresh Fig Custard Ice Cream

Inspired by techniques from Serious Eats.

Custard-base (French) · Fig · French

A twist on our classic custard: ripe fresh figs are cooked down into a lemony jam and blended into a slightly lighter yolk custard. Sweet, honeyed and a pretty dusky pink.

Cook35 min
