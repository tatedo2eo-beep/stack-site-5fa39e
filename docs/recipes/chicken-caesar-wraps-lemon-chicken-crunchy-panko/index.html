Chicken Caesar Wraps with Lemon Chicken and Crunchy Panko

Inspired by techniques from NYT Cooking and America's Test Kitchen.

Wraps · Chicken Caesar · American

Thin chicken cutlets are seared to 74 °C and sliced, romaine is tossed in a creamy anchovy-Parmesan dressing, and both are rolled into warmed tortillas with toasted panko.
