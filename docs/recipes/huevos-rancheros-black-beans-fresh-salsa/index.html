Huevos Rancheros with Refried Black Beans, Ranchero Sauce and Fresh Salsa

Inspired by techniques from NYT Cooking and America's Test Kitchen.

Huevos rancheros · Classic · Mexican

A fresh tomato and jalapeño salsa is split three ways: cooked into ranchero sauce, mashed into smoky black beans and served fresh, all around eggs with set yolks on corn tortillas.

Cook35 min

Ingredients

Metric · 4 servings

Salsa

  • 700 g ripe tomatoes, diced
  • ½ medium white onion, finely chopped about 75 g
  • 1 jalapeño, stemmed and finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 15 g coriander, chopped, plus leaves to serve
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1.5 g fine sea salt or table salt about ¼ tsp

Beans and eggs

  • 4 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 400 g tins black beans, drained (about 480 g), plus 120 ml water
  • ¾ tsp ground cumin
  • ¼ tsp smoked paprika
  • 8 large eggs

To serve

  • 8 small corn tortillas (15 cm), warmed
  • 1 ripe avocado, sliced
  • 50 g queso fresco or feta, crumbled (check the rennet)

Method

  1. 1

    Make the salsa. Mix the tomatoes, onion, jalapeño, lime juice, half the garlic and the salt in the large bowl. Set aside 360 ml as fresh salsa.

  2. 2

    Cook the ranchero sauce. Put the rest in the saucepan and cook over medium-high heat for 10 minutes, stirring often, until soft and thickened. Tip it into a bowl and cover to keep warm.

  3. 3

    Make the beans. Rinse the pan and heat 2 tbsp oil over medium heat. Add the remaining garlic and stir for 30 seconds, then add the beans, water, cumin, paprika and 120 ml of the fresh salsa. Cook for 8-10 minutes, mashing, until thick and smooth. Keep warm.

  4. 4

    Cook the eggs. Heat 1 tbsp oil in the frying pan over medium heat, crack in 4 eggs, add 1 tbsp water, cover and cook for 5-6 minutes until the whites are firm and the yolks are set through (71 °C). The steam under the lid sets the tops without crisping the bases too much. Keep warm and repeat with the remaining oil and eggs.

  5. 5

    Warm the tortillas. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for 30 seconds each side and wrap them in the tea towel so they stay soft.

  6. 6

    Assemble. Put 2 tortillas on each plate, spread with the beans, top each with an egg and spoon the ranchero sauce around. Stir the coriander into the remaining fresh salsa and add it with the avocado, cheese and coriander leaves.

Common mistake

Cold, stiff tortillas are the commonest mistake; warm them until soft and pliable.

More from the cat

  • Taste the beans before adding any salt, as tinned beans vary.
  • Remove the jalapeño seeds for a milder salsa.
  • Slice the avocado at the last minute so it stays green.

Storage

Store the sauce, beans and fresh salsa separately in the fridge for up to 2 days. Reheat the sauce and beans until piping hot, and cook the eggs fresh. Cool leftovers in a shallow container and refrigerate within 2 hours (within 1 hour on a day above 32°C).

Equipment

  • large bowl
  • medium saucepan
  • potato masher
  • 30 cm non-stick frying pan with a lid
  • fish slice
  • clean tea towel or foil for the tortillas
  • instant-read probe thermometer
