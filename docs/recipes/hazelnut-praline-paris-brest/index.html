Hazelnut Praline Paris-Brest

Inspired by techniques from NYT Cooking and America's Test Kitchen.

Paris-Brest · French

A crisp choux ring scattered with hazelnuts, split and piped with a light hazelnut praline buttercream, then dusted with icing sugar.

Cook1 h
Total2 h
DifficultyHard

⚠️ Food safety

Don't taste the raw dough or batter: raw flour and raw egg aren't safe to eat until baked.

Ingredients

Metric · 8 servings

Praline

  • 140 g blanched hazelnuts, toasted
  • 100 g caster sugar
  • 60 ml water
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1.5 g fine sea salt or table salt about ¼ tsp
  • 1 tbsp sunflower oil

Choux ring

  • 85 g unsalted butter, diced
  • 90 ml water
  • 90 ml whole milk
  • 6 g caster sugar about 1½ tsp
  • 1.5 g fine sea salt or table salt about ¼ tsp
  • 125 g plain flour, sifted
  • 4 large eggs, beaten
  • 30 g blanched hazelnuts, chopped

Praline cream

  • 360 ml whole milk
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 70 g caster sugar
  • 30 g cornflour (cornstarch)
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 200 g soft unsalted butter
  • 12 g icing sugar, for dusting about 1 tbsp

Method

  1. 1

    Praline. boil the sugar, water and lemon juice without stirring until deep golden, 10-12 min. Stir in the toasted hazelnuts, pour onto the oiled paper and leave 30 min to harden. Break up and process to a fine powder, then add the salt and oil and process 2-3 min to a smooth paste. Take the caramel off when it is deep amber; pale caramel tastes only sweet.

  2. 2

    Choux. heat the oven to 200 °C (180 °C fan) and draw a 20 cm circle on the underside of a sheet of baking paper on the tray. Bring the butter, water, milk, sugar and salt to the boil, beat in the flour, then cook on low 2 min until shiny. Cool 4 min and beat in the egg gradually to a glossy paste that falls in a V.

  3. 3

    Pipe and bake. pipe a thick ring on the circle, a second ring inside it touching, and a third on top along the join. Scatter with the chopped hazelnuts and bake 40-45 min until deep golden and firm, then turn the oven off and leave 30 min with the door ajar. Cool.

  4. 4

    Cream. whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour; heat the milk to a simmer and whisk it in. Return to the pan and whisk over medium heat until it bubbles for 1 min, past 85 °C. Stir in the vanilla, cover and cool to room temperature, about 1 h.

  5. 5

    Whip. beat the butter until pale and fluffy, then beat in the cooled cream a spoonful at a time, and finally 150 g of the praline paste. If the cream looks split, keep beating; it comes together as it warms slightly.

  6. 6

    Assemble. split the ring in half horizontally with the serrated knife. Pipe the praline cream in tall swirls over the base, put the lid on and dust with icing sugar. Chill 30 min and serve within 4 h.

Common mistake

A common mistake is adding cold pastry cream to the butter, which splits it; both should be at room temperature.

More from the cat

  • Leftover praline paste keeps for 1 month in a jar.
  • Dry the ring well in the oven so it holds the heavy filling.

Storage

Keep in the fridge and eat within 1 day; take it out 20 min before serving so the cream softens.
