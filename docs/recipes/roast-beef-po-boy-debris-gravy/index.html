Roast Beef Po’ Boy with Debris Gravy

Inspired by techniques from Serious Eats.

Po' boy · Roast beef · American (New Orleans)

Beef braised low in the oven with vegetables, stock and herbs, chilled overnight, sliced thin and reheated in a nutty roux gravy made from the cooking liquid. Piled into French bread with mayonnaise, tomato and lettuce.
