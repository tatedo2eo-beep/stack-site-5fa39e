Canh Chua Cá: Sour Fish Soup with Snapper, Tomato and Pineapple

Inspired by techniques from Serious Eats and NYT Cooking.

Canh chua · Fish · Vietnamese

A quick, bright canh chua with chunks of white fish, a cooked-down tomato base, tamarind and lime, pineapple and okra, topped with bean sprouts, spring onion and coriander.

Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Total40 min
DifficultyEasy
Per serving293 kcal

Ingredients

Ingredient names · quantities with Premium

  • skinless snapper or other firm white fish fillet, in 4 cm chunks
  • fish sauce, for the fish
  • neutral oil
  • shallots, thinly sliced
  • garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • ripe tomato, finely chopped, plus 2 more cut into wedges
  • water
  • tamarind paste (concentrate-free pulp, sieved)
  • sugar
  • fish sauce
  • lime juice
  • fresh pineapple, in small wedges
  • okra pods, cut in thick pieces
  • bean sprouts
  • spring onions, sliced
  • coriander leaves
