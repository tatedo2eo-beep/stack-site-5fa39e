Raspberry Thumbprint Cookies

Inspired by techniques from King Arthur Baking, America's Test Kitchen and NYT Cooking.

Thumbprints · Raspberry · American

These raspberry thumbprint cookies follow the classic method, filled with warmed seedless raspberry jam instead of lemon curd and finished with an optional lemon-icing drizzle once cooled.
