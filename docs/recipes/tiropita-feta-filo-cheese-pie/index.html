Tiropita: Greek Feta and Filo Cheese Pie

Inspired by techniques from America's Test Kitchen.

Tiropita · Classic · Greek

A golden, shatteringly crisp filo pie with a tangy filling of feta, nutty graviera and cottage cheese set with egg, baked in a square tin and cut into nine.

Prep30 min
Cook30 min
Total1 h
DifficultyMedium
Per serving328 kcal
FreeVegetarianNo added sugar
Servings9Recipe makes 9

Ingredients

Metric · 9 servings

Filling

  • 280 g feta in brine, drained and crumbled
  • 60 g graviera or Gruyère, coarsely grated
  • 115 g full-fat cottage cheese
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 tsp cornflour
  • ½ tsp dried mint (optional)
  • ¼ tsp ground black pepper

Pastry

  • 16 filo sheets, about 35 x 23 cm, thawed about 300 g; cut larger sheets to fit
  • 140 g unsalted butter, melted

Method

  1. 1

    Filling. Mix the feta, graviera, cottage cheese, eggs, cornflour, mint and pepper in a bowl. The cornflour holds the moisture from the cheeses, so the filling sets creamy rather than weeping.

  2. 2

    Prepare. Put a rack in the lowest position of the oven and heat it to 220 °C (200 °C fan). Line the tin with two crossed strips of foil that overhang the sides, then brush it with butter. Cover the filo with a damp tea towel so it does not dry out and crack.

  3. 3

    Base. Lay in 8 filo sheets one at a time, brushing each with butter and turning each a quarter turn from the last so the overhang falls on all four sides. The butter between the layers fries them into crisp flakes in the oven.

  4. 4

    Fill. Spread the filling evenly over the base, right into the corners.

  5. 5

    Top. Layer 8 more sheets over the filling in the same way, brushing each one with butter.

  6. 6

    Seal. Pinch the overhanging edges together and roll them inwards to make a thick rim, then brush the top and rim with the rest of the butter. Cut through the top layers into 9 squares without cutting the base; pre-cutting stops the crisp top shattering when you serve.

  7. 7

    Bake. Bake for 25-30 minutes, until deep golden and crisp and the centre of the filling reads 71 °C. Heat from the low rack crisps the base so it does not go soggy.

  8. 8

    Rest. Lift the pie out on the foil onto a rack, slide the foil away and leave for 30 minutes. As it cools the filling firms up, so the squares cut cleanly; cut along the marks and serve warm.

Common mistake

Letting the filo dry out is the common mistake; keep the sheets covered and work steadily.

More from the cat

  • Torn sheets are fine in the middle layers, so save the best ones for the top.
  • Drain the feta well so the filling is not wet.
  • Bake until deep golden; pale filo is soft and greasy.

Storage

Keep leftovers covered in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat at 180 °C for 10-12 minutes, until piping hot and crisp again. Freeze the unbaked pie, well wrapped, for up to 1 month and bake from frozen, adding 10-15 minutes. Cool leftovers in a shallow container and refrigerate within 2 hours (within 1 hour on a day above 32°C).

  • foil

  • mixing bowl
  • sharp knife
  • wire rack
  • instant-read thermometer
