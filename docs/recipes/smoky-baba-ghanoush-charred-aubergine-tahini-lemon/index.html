Smoky Baba Ghanoush with Charred Aubergine, Tahini and Lemon

Inspired by techniques from NYT Cooking and America's Test Kitchen.

Baba ghanoush · Classic · Levantine

Whole aubergines are charred until completely soft, drained, chopped and whisked into a lemon-garlic base with tahini, a little yoghurt and olive oil.

Prep15 min
Cook40 min
Total55 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

Metric · 6 servings

Aubergine

  • 2 medium aubergines (about 700 g) about 350 g each

Dressing

  • 2 tbsp lemon juice plus extra to taste
  • 1 garlic cloves, grated to a paste
  • 4.5 g fine sea salt or table salt about ¾ tsp
  • 60 g tahini, well stirred
  • 20 g plain whole-milk yoghurt
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil

To serve

  • 5 g flat-leaf parsley, chopped

Method

  1. 1

    Char the aubergines. Prick each aubergine 6 times. Grill on a foil-lined tray as close to a very hot grill as possible, or turn over a gas flame or barbecue, for 30-40 minutes, turning every 10 minutes, until blackened and completely soft.

  2. 2

    Drain. Put the aubergines in a colander and leave for 20 minutes to cool and drain off the bitter juices.

  3. 3

    Mellow the garlic. Meanwhile stir the lemon juice, garlic and salt together in the bowl.

  4. 4

    Chop. Split the aubergines, scoop out the flesh and discard the skin and stems. Chop the flesh finely and add it to the bowl.

  5. 5

    Mix. Whisk in the tahini, yoghurt and 2 tbsp oil. Leave 20 minutes for the flavours to blend, then add more lemon to taste.

  6. 6

    Serve. Spread in a shallow bowl, drizzle with the remaining oil and scatter with parsley. Serve with warm pita.

Cat tip

The blacker the skin, the smokier the dip; a gas flame or barbecue gives the most flavour.

More from the cat

  • Draining the flesh keeps the dip thick and stops it tasting bitter.
  • Chopping by hand gives a better texture than a food processor.

Storage

Keep covered in the fridge for up to 4 days. Do not leave out for more than 2 hours. Cool leftovers in a shallow container and refrigerate within 2 hours (within 1 hour on a day above 32°C).

Equipment

  • grill or gas hob, or an oven with a grill
  • foil-lined baking tray
  • tongs
  • colander
  • medium bowl
  • sharp knife and chopping board
  • spoon and whisk
