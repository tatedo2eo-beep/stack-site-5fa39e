Vanilla Semifreddo with Raspberry Swirl

Inspired by techniques from America's Test Kitchen.

Semifreddo · Classic · Italian

A chilled vanilla dessert featuring vibrant raspberry jam ripples and a light, airy texture achieved through careful whisking and folding.

Prep30 min
Cook10 min
Total40 min
DifficultyMedium
Per serving304 kcal

Ingredients

Metric · 8 servings

Semifreddo

  • 360 ml cold double cream
  • 4 large eggs
  • 150 g caster sugar
  • 1 g fine sea salt or table salt about ⅛ tsp
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Swirl

  • 120 g seedless raspberry jam about 6 tbsp

Method

  1. 1

    Line. Line a 23 x 13 cm loaf tin with cling film. Ensure there is an 8 cm overhang on the sides for easy removal.

  2. 2

    Whip. Whip 360 ml cold double cream to soft peaks. Start on medium-low until foamy then increase to high for 1-3 min and chill.

  3. 3

    Heat. Whisk 4 large eggs with 150 g caster sugar and ⅛ tsp fine salt in a heatproof bowl set over barely simmering water, with the bowl clear of the water. Keep whisking until the mixture reaches 71 °C, 5-8 min.

  4. 4

    Beat. Transfer to a mixer bowl and add 1 tsp vanilla. Beat on medium-high for 3-4 min until the mixture is thick, pale and doubled in volume.

  5. 5

    Fold. Gently fold in half of the chilled cream using a silicone spatula. Add the remaining cream and fold until no streaks remain.

  6. 6

    Swirl. Spread a third of the mixture in the tin, dot with 2 tbsp jam and swirl it through with a skewer. Add half the remaining mixture and 2 tbsp jam and swirl again, then finish with the rest of the mixture and the last 2 tbsp jam.

  7. 7

    Freeze. Smooth the top, cover with the overhanging film and freeze for at least 8 h until firm.

  8. 8

    Serve. Turn the semifreddo out onto a board and peel off the film. Cut 2.5 cm slices with a knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry, and serve at once.

Cat tip

Use a skewer to create a decorative marble effect through the different layers of jam.

More from the cat

  • Ensure the heatproof bowl does not touch the simmering water to prevent the eggs from scrambling.
  • Dip your sharp knife in hot water and wipe it dry between each slice for clean edges.

Storage

Wrap well and keep in the freezer up to 2 weeks; slice straight from the freezer with a hot, dry knife.

Equipment

  • 23 x 13 cm loaf tin
  • cling film
  • stand mixer with whisk
  • heatproof bowl
  • saucepan
  • instant-read thermometer
  • silicone spatula
  • skewer
  • sharp knife
