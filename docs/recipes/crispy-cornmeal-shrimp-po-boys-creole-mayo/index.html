Crispy Cornmeal Shrimp Po' Boys with Creole Mayo

Inspired by techniques from NYT Cooking and America's Test Kitchen.

Po' boy · Shrimp · American

Prawns are coated in seasoned flour, egg and a Creole cornmeal mix, chilled, deep-fried until golden and opaque, then piled into rolls with Creole mayo, lettuce, tomato and pickles.

Prep25 min
