Tofu Pad Thai

Inspired by techniques from NYT Cooking and America's Test Kitchen.

Pad thai · Tofu · Thai

This vegetarian pad thai follows the classic prawn and tofu method, but leaves out the prawns and fish sauce, uses soy sauce in the tamarind sauce, and fries a larger batch of pressed tofu until crisp.

Prep40 min
Cook20 min
Total1 h
DifficultyMedium
Per serving702 kcal

Ingredients

Ingredient names · quantities with Premium

  • dried flat rice noodles (3-5 mm wide)
  • tamarind pulp (from a block)
  • boiling water
  • palm sugar, chopped, or light brown sugar
  • light soy sauce
  • neutral oil (rapeseed, sunflower or vegetable)
  • extra-firm tofu, patted dry, in 1.5 cm cubes
  • garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • shallots, finely chopped
  • large eggs, beaten
  • bean sprouts
  • garlic chives or spring onion greens, cut into 3 cm lengths
  • roasted unsalted peanuts, chopped
  • lime, cut into wedges
  • chilli flakes, to serve
