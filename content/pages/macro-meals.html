Macro Meals

Coming soon. This section is being written.

Macro Meals will be new recipes built for high protein: every one will have at least 10 g of protein per 100 kcal, with flavour first.

What's planned

  • Full nutrition per serving, in grams and kcal.
  • Option buttons on each recipe: lactose-free, low or no sugar, lower fat, less spicy, vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free. No sweeteners.
  • Links from the daily needs calculator, so you can see how a meal fits your protein target.
