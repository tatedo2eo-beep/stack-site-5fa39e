Who we are

{{BRAND}} is a recipe website. It is currently a static informational site: there are no user accounts, no logins, and no payments on the site at this time.

Information we collect today

Because the site is static and does not have accounts or payments yet, we collect very little personal data directly. The sections below describe exactly what happens today.

Information stored only in your browser

  • If you mark recipes as favourites, that list is saved using your browser's local storage (localStorage) on your own device.
  • If you choose a look (one of the 5 designs and 3 colour palettes), that choice is also saved using localStorage on your own device (keys design and pal; favourites use fav).
  • A test-only "premium preview" switch, used while the site is being built, is stored the same way (preview).
  • These are only set when you use the feature, and they are needed to give you what you asked for, so they do not need consent. We do not set any cookies.
  • This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us, never stored on our servers, and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

Hosting provider logs

The site is currently hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and is planned to move to Cloudflare Pages. Fonts and images are served from our own site; no third-party scripts are loaded.

  • Our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information for every visit, such as your IP address, browser/user agent, the page requested, and the date and time.
  • These logs exist for security purposes, such as detecting abuse, attacks, or technical problems, and are handled by our hosting provider in the ordinary course of serving the website.
  • We do not use this information for marketing or profiling.

Cookies and analytics

  • At this time, the site sets no cookies at all: no analytics cookies and no advertising cookies.
  • We do not currently run any tracking, analytics or advertising scripts.
  • If we add visitor statistics, we plan to use a privacy-friendly, cookieless service (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) that counts page views in aggregate without cookies, local storage or fingerprinting. We will name the service here before it is switched on.

Contact

  • If you email us, or use the form on the contact page (it simply opens your own email app with your message filled in), we receive your name, email address and whatever you write. Nothing is sent or stored by the website itself.
  • We use this only to reply to you and to fix what you report, and keep it no longer than needed for that.

Calculator

  • The calculator works in your browser. The details you enter (such as age, height, weight and activity) are used to work out the results on your own device and are not sent to us or stored by us. If that ever changes, we will say so here first.

Planned features

We are actively developing {{BRAND}}. Before any of the features below are switched on, we will update this Privacy Policy and ask for your consent where required by law (for example, through a cookie/consent banner for visitors in the EU and UK).

  • Advertising. We plan to show ads using Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites; Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads based on those visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before any advertising cookies are set, and can change their choice at any time.
  • Accounts and Premium subscriptions. We plan to introduce optional user accounts and a paid "Premium" subscription. Payments for Premium are planned to be processed by Lemon Squeezy, acting as the Merchant of Record. This means Lemon Squeezy collects and processes your payment details (such as card information) directly; we never see or store your full card number. We would only receive limited information needed to manage your subscription, such as your email address and subscription status.
  • AI cooking assistant. We plan to add an AI-powered cooking assistant. Questions or prompts you type into this assistant are expected to be sent to a third-party AI provider for processing in order to generate a response. We will name the provider and explain this clearly, and ask for consent where required, before this feature is enabled.

How we use information

  • To operate and maintain the website.
  • To keep the website secure and to investigate misuse, using hosting provider logs.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Legal basis and your rights (EU/UK visitors)

If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, data protection law (such as the GDPR or UK GDPR) gives you certain rights. Depending on the data involved, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to erase your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Ask for your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (for example, future cookie consent).

Because the site currently has no accounts and does not collect personal data directly from you (other than hosting logs), there is usually little personal data to access or erase today. You can still contact us with any request and we will respond.

Data retention

  • Local storage data (favourites, theme) stays on your device until you clear it yourself; we do not control or set a retention period for it.
  • Hosting provider logs are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for security purposes, as determined by our hosting provider.

International data

We are based in Egypt and our audience is worldwide. Our hosting provider may process logs in countries other than your own. When we introduce accounts, payments, or AI features, we will provide further detail about any international transfers and the safeguards used.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children. It is not intended for use by children under 13 years old, and in the European Union/UK, not intended for children under 16 without appropriate consent. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website changes, in particular when we launch advertising, accounts, Premium subscriptions, or the AI assistant. We will update the date at the top of this page when we do.
