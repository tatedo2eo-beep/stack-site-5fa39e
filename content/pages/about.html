{{BRAND}} is a small, independent recipe site started by Joe, a young home cook who got tired of recipe pages that bury the method under a life story and measure everything in cups.

What you get here

  • Metric, always. Grams, millilitres, teaspoons and tablespoons. No cups, no ounces.
  • Straight to the point. Ingredients, equipment, numbered steps, and a cook mode that keeps your screen on.
  • Honest numbers. Nutrition per serving estimated from USDA FoodData Central, the EU 14 allergens, and diet tags that are checked against the ingredients.
  • Labels you can check. High Protein and WHO Healthy are calculated by the site from the numbers, and every recipe shows its working.

Original recipes

Every recipe is written for this site. Photos are generated or shot for the recipe they illustrate.
