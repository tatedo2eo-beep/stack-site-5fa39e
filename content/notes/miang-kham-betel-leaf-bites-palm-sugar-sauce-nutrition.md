## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Serves 6 as a snack, about 5 leaves each.
- Dried shrimp are sold ready to eat; the sauce is boiled.
- Lime peel is eaten here; if it tastes too bitter, serve lime wedges to squeeze instead.
- If the diced ginger is too fiery, rinse it in cold water a few times and pat dry.
