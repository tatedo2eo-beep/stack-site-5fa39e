## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Makes about 24 fritters, 3 a serving.
- No salt is added; the soaked cod seasons the mix. Soaking times vary by thickness: taste a flake of the cooked cod, and if it is still very salty, soak the next batch longer.
- The egg-bound centre is fried to 74 °C.
- Frying oil absorbed is estimated at 40 g for the batch.
