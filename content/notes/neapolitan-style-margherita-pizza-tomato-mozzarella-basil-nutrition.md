## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- One 25 cm pizza per person.
- Home ovens cannot reach a wood-fired oven's 450 °C; a stone or steel heated 45-60 min plus the grill gives a blistered rim in 5-7 min.
- Dough salt kept at 1.5% of the flour (traditional doughs use 2.5-3%) to keep each pizza under 3 g salt.
- Mozzarella, cheddar and Parmesan are often made with animal rennet; choose cheese made with microbial or vegetarian rennet if needed.
