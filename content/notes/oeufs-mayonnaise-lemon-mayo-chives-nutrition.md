## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Egg safety: the mayonnaise yolk is raw, so use a pasteurised yolk. Otherwise whisk the yolk with the lemon juice and 1 tbsp water in a heatproof bowl over barely simmering water until it reaches 71 °C and thickens, cool for 5 min, then continue. Pregnant people, young children, older adults and anyone with weakened immunity should skip raw-yolk mayonnaise.
- A serving is 2 egg halves with about 40 g mayonnaise.
