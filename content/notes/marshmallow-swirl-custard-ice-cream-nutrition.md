## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Vanilla is alcohol-free vanilla extract or paste.
- An ice-cream maker is needed; freeze a canister-style bowl for at least 24 h first.
- The marshmallow swirl uses egg whites whipped with syrup at 115 °C; this heats them but does not reliably pasteurise them, so use pasteurised egg whites for anyone pregnant, very young, elderly or with low immunity.
