## Assumptions

- Curry paste saltiness varies a lot by brand; this uses about 3 g sodium per 100 g. Taste before adding any more fish sauce.
- Many massaman pastes contain shrimp paste; some Hanafi followers avoid it, so choose a paste without it if needed.
