## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Makes about 250 ml; a serving is about 2 tbsp with toasts.
- Classic raw-egg method: use pasteurised eggs. Pregnant women, babies and young children, older people and anyone unwell should skip it.
- Optional cooked-yolk version: whisk the yolks with 1 tbsp lemon juice and 1 tbsp water over barely simmering water to 71 °C, cool 5 min, then whisk in the garlic paste and the oil.
