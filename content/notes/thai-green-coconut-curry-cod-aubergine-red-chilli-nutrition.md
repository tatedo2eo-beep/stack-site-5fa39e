## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Curry paste saltiness varies a lot by brand; this uses about 3 g sodium per 100 g. Taste before adding any more fish sauce.
- Many Thai curry pastes contain shrimp paste; some Hanafi followers avoid it, so choose a paste without it if needed.
- Thai round aubergines are sold in Asian grocers; ordinary aubergine works and takes the same time.
- Nutrition does not include rice.
