## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Georgian imeruli and sulguni cheeses are hard to find; mozzarella and feta give the right melt and tang.
- Egg is cooked until set (71 °C) rather than left runny.
- Mozzarella, cheddar, Swiss, feta and Parmesan are often made with animal rennet; choose cheese made with microbial or vegetarian rennet (or halal-certified) if needed.
