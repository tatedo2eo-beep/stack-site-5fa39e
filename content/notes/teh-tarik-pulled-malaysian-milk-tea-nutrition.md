## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Caffeine is not part of the nutrition panel; expect roughly 40-70 mg a serving (EFSA guidance: up to 400 mg a day for healthy adults, 200 mg in pregnancy).
- The tea is very hot: start low, pull slowly and keep children away while pulling.
- Double cream can replace the evaporated milk; add an extra ½ tbsp condensed milk.
