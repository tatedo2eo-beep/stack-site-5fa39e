## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Caffeine is not part of the nutrition panel; a double shot has roughly 120-150 mg (EFSA guidance: up to 400 mg a day for healthy adults, 200 mg in pregnancy).
- No espresso machine? Brew 40 ml of strong coffee in a moka pot. It will be espresso-style, with little crema.
- No steam wand? Heat the milk to 60 °C, then froth it with a handheld frother for 30-45 s, or shake it in a half-full lidded jar for 30 s and microwave it uncovered for 30 s.
