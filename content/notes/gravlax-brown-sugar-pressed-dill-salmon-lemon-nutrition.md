## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Raw fish: buy sushi- or sashimi-grade fish from a fishmonger you trust, frozen first (-20 °C for 7 days or -35 °C for 15 h) to kill parasites; keep it cold and eat it within 3 days of curing.
- The splash of spirit some versions use is replaced by lemon juice.
- Gravlax is cured, not cooked; the salt and sugar absorbed are estimated at about 3% and 2.5% of the fish weight.
