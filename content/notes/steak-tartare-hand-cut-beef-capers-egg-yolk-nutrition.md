## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.

- Pasteurised egg yolks are specified because the yolk is eaten raw.
- Lemon juice replaces the usual splash of vinegar; the optional fish sauce is left out.
- Starter portions of about 100 g beef each.
