## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Caffeine is not part of the nutrition panel; expect roughly 60-70 mg (about 2 g matcha) a serving (EFSA guidance: up to 400 mg a day for healthy adults, 200 mg in pregnancy).
- Water just off the boil makes matcha bitter; let the kettle cool for 3-4 minutes, or mix 1 part boiling with 1 part cold water.
