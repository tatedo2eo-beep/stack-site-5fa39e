## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Caffeine is not part of the nutrition panel; expect roughly 100-200 mg (Vietnamese blends often include robusta, which is higher in caffeine) a serving (EFSA guidance: up to 400 mg a day for healthy adults, 200 mg in pregnancy).
- Coffee grounds are not counted; the coffee is counted as brewed coffee.
- Drink it hot by skipping the ice (cà phê sữa nóng).
