## Assumptions
- Salt: 1 tsp fine salt taken as 6 g.
- Makes about 400 ml aioli; a serving is about 2 tbsp aioli with vegetables, with aioli left over.
- Classic raw-egg method: use pasteurised eggs. Pregnant women, babies and young children, older people and anyone unwell should skip it.
- Optional cooked-yolk version: whisk the egg with the lemon juice and zest and 1 tbsp water in a heatproof bowl over barely simmering water until it reads 71 °C and thickens, cool for 5 min, then add the oil as written.
