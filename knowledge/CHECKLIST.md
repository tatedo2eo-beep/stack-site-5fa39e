# Recipe Review Checklist

> **Private. For writing and reviewing site recipes.** Each line points to the knowledge file with the evidence. A recipe passes only when every applicable box is ticked. Joe can't test-cook, so **any unticked box is a rewrite, not a judgement call**.

## 1. Ingredients list
- [ ] Every ingredient used in the method appears in the list, and every listed ingredient is used. Watch for "salt/pepper/oil for the pan", pasta water, garnish and finishing butter or acid.
- [ ] **Weights in grams** for flour, sugar, butter, cheese, meat, pasta and salt. Volumes are secondary. Eggs are given by size (large) and, for baking, by weight.
- [ ] **The salt is named** (fine sea, table or kosher brand) with **grams**. Volume alone is not enough (06-G1).
- [ ] Ingredient state is given: softened butter at 18–24 °C, room-temperature eggs and dairy, cold butter for pastry, finely grated cheese (05-CK2, 04-P5).
- [ ] Pan or tin size and material is given (metal, not glass, for brownies and cakes; 03-K8, 05-CK1).

## 2. Seasoning % (calculate it)
- [ ] Add up the total food weight, then work out the salt % (06-G1):
  - lean meat **≈ 0.5 %**
  - mid-fat **≈ 0.75 %**
  - burgers or 80/20 **0.8–1 %** of the raw beef weight, on the surface (01-B3)
  - soups and sauces **≈ 1 %**
  - pasta water **1 %**, or **0.5 %** if the sauce is salty (04-P1)
- [ ] Reduced sauces: salt is adjusted **after** reduction, not front-loaded (06-G1).
- [ ] Salty components (Pecorino, guanciale, soy, stock, anchovy) are counted against the salt total.
- [ ] Cookies and cakes: salt is **≈ 1.2–2 % of flour weight** (03-K1).
- [ ] "Season to taste" appears **only** where tasting is safe. Raw meat mixes and batters get exact grams (06-G1).
- [ ] An acid, fat and texture check is done: a rich dish has an acid or fresh element (06-G2).

## 3. Safe temperatures (non-negotiable)
- [ ] Ground meat (burgers, meatballs, kofta, meatloaf): **71 °C** core, with a thermometer step. **No pink or medium-rare language** (01-B8, 06-G9).
- [ ] **All poultry 74 °C**, measured before it comes off the heat. No 65 °C breast (02-C1).
- [ ] Whole cuts of beef, pork or lamb **63 °C + 3 min rest** minimum. Fish **63 °C** or the opaque and flaking cue.
- [ ] Egg dishes **71 °C**. Raw or lightly cooked egg (carbonara, mayo, mousse, tiramisu) carries a **pasteurised-egg note** (04-P6).
- [ ] Raw cookie dough or flour carries a "don't eat raw" note (03-K10).
- [ ] **Swiss meringue buttercream:** whites and sugar cooked to **85 °C** and held there. If a recipe uses the 43 °C dissolve-only method, it must carry the pasteurised-egg note. Italian meringue syrup reaches **116–118 °C** (05-CK12).
- [ ] **Hollandaise and béarnaise** carry the pasteurised-egg note. They are served within 1 h and **never cooled and reheated** (06-G11).
- [ ] **Cream cheese frosting:** frosted cakes are **refrigerated**, with a maximum of about 4 h at room temperature. Ganache fillings and filled macarons are chilled (05-CK12, CK13; 03-K13).
- [ ] **Stock and cooked rice:** chilled quickly. Stock is used within **2 days** or frozen; rice follows the 3–4 day leftover rule. Both are reheated to 74 °C (06-G10, G12, G9).
- [ ] Storage and make-ahead lines: refrigerate within **2 h (1 h above 32 °C)**, leftovers keep **3–4 days**, reheat to **74 °C**. Thaw and marinate in the fridge (06-G9).
- [ ] Frying: thermometer, oil temperature stated (usually 165–175 °C), dry food, never unattended, the **no-water grease fire** line, and the internal temperature checked out of the oil (06-G6, 02-C6).

## 4. Ratios (sanity check against the knowledge files)
- [ ] **Cookies:** butter 60–80 % of flour, sugar 85–105 %, baking soda ≈ 1 % (03-K1).
- [ ] **Butter cake:** fat, sugar, egg and flour near equal, flour not much above the egg weight. Self-raising flour plus at most about 1 tsp baking powder per 225 g. Pans no more than ⅔ full (05-CK1).
- [ ] **Fresh egg pasta:** egg ≈ 60–70 % of flour weight (04-P10).
- [ ] **Pasta water:** 2–3 L per 450 g dried pasta. At least 240 ml reserved (04-P2, P3).
- [ ] **Vinaigrette:** 2–3 : 1 oil to acid, plus an emulsifier (06-G7).
- [ ] **Burger:** fat 20–25 % (80/20) (01-B1).
- [ ] **Sauces:** carbonara about 50 g cheese and 2 eggs + 6 yolks (or 2 + 2) per 340–450 g pasta. Alfredo butter : cheese : fresh pasta ≈ 1 : 1 : 4 (04-P6, P7).
- [ ] **Brine:** check the dry-brine and wet-brine strength and timing against 02-C2 and C3.
  - Dry brine is **0.5–0.7 % salt by meat weight** (pass-2 correction).
- [ ] **Blondies:** melted butter, light brown sugar, sugar ≈ flour by weight, 175 °C. A 13×9 pan bakes about 22–25 min (03-K9).
- [ ] **Shortbread:** butter : sugar : starch ≈ **2 : 1 : 2–3**, no egg, no leavener, baked at 160–170 °C (03-K11).
- [ ] **Sugar cookies (cut-out):** rolled 3–6 mm between parchment, chilled dough and chilled cut-outs, 175 °C (03-K12).
- [ ] **Macarons:** almond flour ≈ icing sugar by weight. Italian syrup at 118 °C. Oven 140–160 °C (house 150 °C) (03-K13).
- [ ] **Genoise:** butter : sugar : flour : eggs ≈ **1 : 2 : 2 : 4**, flour 70 % AP + 30 % cornstarch, 175 °C (05-CK11).
- [ ] **Buttercreams:**
  - American ≈ 1 : 1.7 butter : icing sugar.
  - SMBC finishes at about 22 °C.
  - IMBC whites : sugar : butter ≈ 1 : 2 : 3.
  - Cream cheese frosting uses brick, full-fat cream cheese only (05-CK12).
- [ ] **Chocolate:** the tempering curve matches 05-CK13. Dark: melt 50–55 °C, cool to 28–29 °C, work at 31–32 °C; milk and white melt at ≤ 45 °C. Ganache chocolate : cream by weight: **2 : 1** truffles, **about 1.1 : 1** slab, **about 0.9 : 1** soft filling.
- [ ] **Roux and béchamel:** equal fat and flour. Per 240 ml liquid: **1½ tbsp (12 g) flour** for standard, 1–2 tbsp for thin, 3 tbsp for thick. A dark roux thickens less (06-G11).
- [ ] **Rice:** long-grain pilaf 1 : 1.5, jasmine 1 : 1.25 by volume. **Water is NOT scaled linearly**: a double pilaf is 3 cups rice : 3¾ cups water (06-G12).
- [ ] **Stock:** 1.8–3.6 kg chicken per 3.8 L water, with wings or feet for body (06-G10).

## 5. Doneness cues present (every cooking step needs one)
- [ ] Each heat step has **time + temperature or heat level + a sensory cue**. For example: "until golden at the edges, 10–12 min"; "until a thermometer reads 71 °C"; "until translucent, about 6 min".
- [ ] **Cakes:** edges pulling away, a clean skewer and spring-back; optionally a core of 93–99 °C (05-CK8).
- [ ] **Cheesecake:** a wobbly centre 5–8 cm across, or a core of 63–68 °C. The cooling and chilling time is stated (≥ 4 h, ideally overnight) (05-CK10).
- [ ] **Cookies:** golden edges and soft centres, then cooled on the sheet (03-K6). **Brownies:** a crackly top and moist crumbs (03-K8).
- [ ] **Blondies:** a shiny, crackled, light-golden top, and a toothpick 5 cm from the side comes out almost clean. **Shortbread:** pale to just golden, and pan shortbread is cut while warm. **Sugar cookies:** edges very lightly golden (03-K9, K11, K12).
- [ ] **Macarons:**
  - The batter falls in a slowly dissolving ribbon.
  - The piped shells rest until they have a matte skin that isn't sticky.
  - They are done when a shell lifts cleanly off the mat (03-K13).
- [ ] **Genoise:** the foam reaches a thick ribbon and about doubles. The baked cake is light gold and springy (05-CK11). **Tempered chocolate:** a test strip sets glossy within about 3–5 min (05-CK13).
- [ ] **Sauces:** béchamel and velouté coat a spoon. Hollandaise coats a spoon but still flows. **Rice:** no water left at the base, then rested 5–10 min (06-G11, G12).
- [ ] **Pasta:** pull about 1 min before the packet time, then finish in the sauce until glossy (04-P3, P4).
- [ ] **Searing:** oil shimmering or lightly smoking, and the crust releases easily (06-G4, G5).
- [ ] **Resting and cooling** times are stated (meat, cakes in the pan about 10 min, chiffon inverted, cheesecake overnight).

## 6. Sequencing errors to catch
- [ ] **Preheat** the oven (≥ 20 min) or the pan is the first step, not mid-recipe.
- [ ] Salt timing: burgers are salted on the **outside just before cooking**, not mixed in early (01-B2). Dry brine gets its full lead time (02-C2).
- [ ] Creaming before the eggs; eggs one at a time; flour **last and just until combined** (05-CK3). Reverse creaming gets the butter into the dry ingredients first (05-CK4).
- [ ] Sugar goes into whites **at the foamy stage**, not before and not at stiff peak (05-CK5).
- [ ] **Cheese is added off the heat** for cacio e pepe, Alfredo and carbonara. Eggs never go into a pan on direct heat (04-P5–P7).
- [ ] **Pasta water is reserved before draining** (04-P3).
- [ ] Aromatics and spices: onion soft **before** garlic and ground spices. Ground spices get about 10 s–2 min in fat, not longer (02-C9, 06-G3).
- [ ] Bolognese: milk is evaporated **before** the wine and tomato (04-P8).
- [ ] Cookie dough rests (≥ 1 h) **before** baking if the recipe relies on it (03-K5).
- [ ] Cake layers cool fully **before** frosting (05-CK9).
- [ ] **Macarons:** rest the piped shells **before** baking. Mature the filled shells for **24–48 h** in the fridge, and bring them to room temperature about 1 h before serving (03-K13).
- [ ] **Meringue buttercreams:** add the butter only after the meringue has cooled to about 32 °C (SMBC) or room temperature (IMBC). Say that a curdled stage is normal and to keep beating (05-CK12).
- [ ] **Genoise:** stir melted butter into about ⅓ of the batter **before** folding it back in. Fold the flour in thirds (05-CK11).
- [ ] **Ganache:** add hot cream in 3–4 additions, then use a blender. Add butter at **35–40 °C** (05-CK13). Keep water away from chocolate.
- [ ] **Stock** stays at a very gentle simmer (no boil), then is strained, chilled and defatted. **Roux** is cooked about 1 min **before** the liquid goes in gradually (06-G10, G11).
- [ ] **Rice:** rinse, then toast in the fat **before** adding hot liquid. Use a towel under the lid during the rest. Risotto gets its butter and cheese **off the heat** (06-G12).
- [ ] Fried food is dried **before** it goes into the oil. Batter and dredge are ready before the oil hits temperature (06-G6).
- [ ] Make-ahead and storage come last and agree with 06-G9.

## 7. Wording and publication rules
- [ ] **No chef names or outlet names** in public copy. Sources stay in /knowledge only.
- [ ] Original wording only: nothing copied from a source recipe's headnote or method.
- [ ] Both units: °C with °F, and grams with cups or spoons where helpful.
- [ ] No unsupported claims ("the only way", "always crack-free"). Where sources disagree, the recipe follows the **safer pick noted in the knowledge file**.
