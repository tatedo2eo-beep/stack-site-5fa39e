## Own cooking judgment
- Ramekins buttered or greased and a little cream added (NYT, ATK)
- Eggs cracked into the ramekins and seasoned (NYT, ATK)
- Baked in a hot water bath until set - here until whites and yolks are fully set (NYT, ATK)
