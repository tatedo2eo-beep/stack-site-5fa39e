## Own cooking judgment
- Salt and coarse pepper rub with an optional uncovered dry-brine (all three); 110-120 °C wood smoke to about 75 °C, then wrapped in butcher paper and cooked to 93-95 °C (NYT 1020184, SE); long rest in a cooler; flat and point sliced separately (NYT 1020184); onion and pickles (NYT 1016755). Salt reduced to keep about 3 g or less per serving.
