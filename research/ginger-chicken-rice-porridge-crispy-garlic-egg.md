## Own cooking judgment
- Garlic fried in oil until golden and drained (SE); bite-size chicken browned (SE; NYT wings); onion, ginger and garlic (SE; NYT); fish sauce (SE; NYT) and black pepper; jasmine rice stirred in (SE; NYT toasted); stock and simmered, stirring, until thick (SE about 30 min; NYT 1.5 h); saffron (NYT); lime or calamansi juice (SE; NYT); spring onions, hard-boiled eggs, fried garlic and lime to serve (SE).
