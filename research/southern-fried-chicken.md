## Own cooking judgment
- 2026-10-22 second-source check (ATK): the frying method and doneness temperature agree. No changes.
- Dry brine (salt + garlic + paprika) and a flour/egg/flour dredge for the Classic.
- Thighs and drumsticks fully fried at 150-160 °C for 13-16 min; Serious Eats (Hattie B's) fries bone-in pieces at about 163 °C for 15-20 min.
- Dark meat to 77-80 °C for tenderness (Serious Eats Nashville uses 80 °C; USDA minimum 74 °C).
