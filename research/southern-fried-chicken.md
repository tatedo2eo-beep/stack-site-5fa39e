## Own cooking judgment
- Dry brine (salt + garlic + paprika) and a flour/egg/flour dredge for the Classic so it differs from the buttermilk flavour.
- Thighs/drumsticks only, fully fried at 150-160 °C for 13-16 min (Bon Appétit 163 °C, 15-20 min for bone-in) instead of Serious Eats' oven finish.
- Dark meat to 77-80 °C for tenderness (Serious Eats Nashville uses 80 °C; USDA minimum 74 °C).
