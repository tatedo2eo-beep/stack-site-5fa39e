## Own cooking judgment
- Warm short-grain rice seasoned with sesame oil and salt (both) and sesame seeds (SE); spinach blanched 45 s, squeezed and seasoned (NYT); carrot cooked briefly with salt (both); thin egg sheet cooked, cooled and cut into strips (both); cucumber strips (SE); rice spread over most of the nori with damp fingers, fillings in rows, rolled firmly with a mat and sealed (both); brushed with sesame oil and sliced (SE). The seasoned beef filling (from the photo), the quick turmeric daikon pickle in place of store-bought pickled radish, the fully set egg and the probe temperatures are my own.
