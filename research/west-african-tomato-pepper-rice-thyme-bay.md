## Sources actually opened
- [Nigerian Jollof Rice (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/nigerian-jollof-rice-recipe-6741471) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Tomatoes, red peppers, onion, Scotch bonnet and stock blended and reduced by half about 30 min; sliced onion, bay, curry powder, thyme, salt and pepper cooked 3 min; tomato paste and butter about 3 min until darker; stew base simmered 15 min until reduced by half; stock added and boiled; converted rice stirred in, sealed with foil and lid, lowest heat 20 min, stirred, 15 min more; sliced tomato, remaining onion and butter; rested 10 min. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Jollof Rice (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020288-jollof-rice) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Tinned tomatoes, red pepper, onion, garlic, ginger and habanero blended and simmered 18-20 min until reduced by a third; onions softened in oil 6-8 min and half reserved; garlic, tomato paste, turmeric and paprika toasted until dark red; sauce, parboiled rice, thyme, bay, salt, pepper and stock; covered and baked 35 min at 350F, rested covered 15 min, fluffed with the reserved onions. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Vegan Jollof Rice (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1018069-vegan-jollof-rice) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Tomatoes, Scotch bonnet, onion and red peppers puréed; oil heated with the purée, salt, curry powder, chilli, garlic and onion powders, bay, ginger and dried thyme and boiled; rice stirred in and cooked covered on low about 45 min, checking at 30 min; rice at the base allowed to char a little for smoky flavour. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
