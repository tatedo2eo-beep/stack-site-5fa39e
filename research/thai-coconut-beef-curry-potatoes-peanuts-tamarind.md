## Own cooking judgment
- Beef browned and braised in water until tender, cooled, and cooking liquid kept (SE; NYT braises in the oven); paste fried in coconut milk added in increments until the oil separates (SE; NYT fries paste 2-3 min); palm sugar, tamarind and fish sauce (both, NYT uses salt); potatoes simmered about 30 min in the sauce (SE); roasted peanuts (NYT). Boneless chuck, cinnamon and star anise in the braise, the reduced paste and fish sauce to keep salt down, and the temperatures are my own.
