# Research: Glossy Candied Orange and Lemon Peel
Dish: Candied citrus peel / flavour: Classic. Private file, not published.

## Sources actually opened
- [How to make candied citrus peel (King Arthur Baking)](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2020/08/05/how-to-make-candied-citrus-peel) [Tier 1 (locked list): King Arthur Baking]: Peel cut into 6 mm strips, blanched in boiling water 5 min three times, then simmered in equal parts sugar and water 40-60 min until soft and translucent, drained and dried on a rack; sugar coating optional. _(curl 200; article read)_
- [Candied Citrus Zest (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/sweet-technique-recipe-candied-citrus-zest) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Zest from 4 citrus fruits blanched three times, then simmered in 1 cup sugar, 1 cup water and 2 tbsp light corn syrup about 20 min until completely translucent. _(curl 200; recipe read)_
- [Candied Orange Peel (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/7564-candied-orange-peel) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Orange peel strips blanched twice, then cooked in sugar syrup until just turning translucent and cooled in the syrup. _(curl 200; recipe read)_
- [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (USDA FSIS)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) [Safety database (locked list): USDA FSIS]: Ground meat 71.1 °C; all poultry incl. ground 73.9 °C; leftovers 73.9 °C. _(opened with WebFetch (curl gets 403 bot-block))_
