## Own cooking judgment
- Shallot, garlic, ginger and chilli fried in oil (both); crab pieces cooked covered in liquid until red and opaque (both), to at least 63 °C by site rule; tomato and chilli sauce, cornflour to thicken, then beaten egg stirred in off the heat (both); spring onions, coriander and buns to match the photo; water replaces broth to keep salt about 3 g; no vinegar or rice wine.
