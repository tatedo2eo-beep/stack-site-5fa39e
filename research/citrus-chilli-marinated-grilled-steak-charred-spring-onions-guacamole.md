## Own cooking judgment
- Steak seasoned with a dried-chilli, citrus, garlic and cumin marinade (SE, NYT) or salt-cumin rub (ATK)
- Marinade wiped off or discarded and steak grilled over very high heat until charred (SE, ATK, NYT)
- Rested about 5 min and sliced thinly against the grain; served with tortillas, lime and guacamole-style garnishes (SE, ATK, NYT)
