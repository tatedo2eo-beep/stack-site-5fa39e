# Research: Pan-Fried Beef and Cabbage Dumplings with Ginger Dipping Sauce
Dish: Potstickers (guotie/jiaozi) / flavour: Classic. Private file, not published.

## Sources actually opened
- [Dumplings With Homemade Wrappers (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/pork-and-leek-dumplings-with-homemade-wrapper) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Hot-water dough of flour and boiling water made in a food processor until smooth and tacky, rested at least 30 min and rolled into wrappers; napa cabbage salted, drained 30 min and squeezed dry; 1 tbsp filling per wrapper, edges moistened, folded and pleated along one edge to seal; dumplings boiled briefly, then pan-fried; dipping sauce of soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, scallion and ginger. (Its pork, Shaoxing wine and Chinkiang vinegar are not used.) _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Beef and Vegetable Potstickers (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/16976-beef-and-vegetable-potstickers) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: 85% lean ground beef stirred vigorously with ginger and soy sauce until sticky, then courgette, carrot, scallions and sesame oil; heaping tablespoon per 10 cm wrapper; browned in oil in a 30 cm nonstick skillet, water added, covered and cooked 3 min, then uncovered over medium-high 1-3 min until the water evaporates and the bottoms are crisp and browned. (Its Shaoxing wine, Sichuan pepper and black-vinegar dipping sauce are not used.) _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Pan-Fried Potstickers with Sesame Beef and Cabbage Filling (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/772-pan-fried-asian-potstickers-with-sesame-beef-and-cabbage-filling) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Napa cabbage tossed with salt and left 15-20 min to wilt, rinsed and squeezed dry; mixed with ground beef, scallion, garlic, soy sauce, sesame seeds and sesame oil; pressed to a flat base and pleated; fried flat side down about 2 min until brown, simmering water added, covered about 3 min until absorbed, then uncovered about 1 min until crisp again. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Fast Pot-Stickers (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/8185-fast-pot-stickers) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Ground meat, minced cabbage, ginger, garlic, scallion whites and soy sauce; rounded teaspoon per wrapper, sealed; fried about 2 min in oil until lightly browned, 1/4 cup water per dozen added, covered and simmered about 3 min, then uncovered and cooked 1-2 min until crisp; dipping sauce of soy, scallion greens and rice vinegar. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_

## Own cooking judgment
- Hot-water dough of flour and just-boiled water kneaded smooth and rested 30 min, then rolled into wrappers (SE); cabbage salted, rested and squeezed dry (ATK 772; SE); beef with ginger and soy sauce stirred until sticky (ATK 16976), with cabbage, scallion, garlic and sesame oil (ATK 772; NYT); edges moistened and pleated into crescents with a flat base (SE; ATK 772); fried flat side down until golden, water added, covered and steamed, then uncovered until the water evaporates and the bases crisp (ATK 16976; ATK 772; NYT); soy and rice vinegar dipping sauce with ginger (NYT; SE). Ginger matchsticks, white pepper, hand-mixing the dough, rolling the edges thinner, steam-frying the homemade wrappers from raw (7-8 min instead of boiling first), quantities and the 71 °C check are my own.
