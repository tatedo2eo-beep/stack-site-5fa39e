## Own cooking judgment
- Fish rubbed with salt and turmeric first (NYT 1020914; NYT 1025936 salted); smooth paste of chilli, coriander, cumin, turmeric, garlic and ginger (SE Goan; NYT 1020914 pounded paste); curry leaves and green chillies spluttered in oil, then onion softened (SE Goan; NYT 1020914 onion and green chilli); paste fried until the oil separates (SE Goan); tomatoes cooked down (NYT 1025936); coconut milk simmered 5 min before the fish (NYT 1025936); fish cooked gently without stirring 5-8 min until opaque and flaky (SE Goan; NYT 1025936); tamarind for sourness (NYT 1020914). Rice as the side (all). Paprika and cayenne stand in for Kashmiri chillies.
