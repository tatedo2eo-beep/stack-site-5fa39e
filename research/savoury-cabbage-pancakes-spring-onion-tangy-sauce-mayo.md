## Sources actually opened
- [Green Okonomiyaki (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020099-green-okonomiyaki) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Water (dashi optional) whisked with eggs, then flour, salt, baking powder and baking soda; cabbage, greens and ginger folded in; half the batter spread into a 15 cm round 2.5 cm thick in 2 tbsp oil without pressing; medium-low 8-10 min until set and golden, inverted with a plate, cooked about 8 min until cooked through in the centre; sauce and mayonnaise to finish. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Osaka-Style Okonomiyaki (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/okonomiyaki-japanese-pancake-cabbage-recipe) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Cabbage, scallions, eggs and water or dashi beaten with flour into a thick, bubbly batter; spread into a 25 cm non-stick pan in hot oil; covered and cooked about 10 min until crisp underneath; flipped using the lid; covered and cooked about 8 min more until not runny in the centre; sauce, mayonnaise and scallion greens to finish. (Bonito, pickled ginger and pork belly are not used.) _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Shrimp Okonomiyaki (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/17541-shrimp-okonomiyaki-japanese-cabbage-pancake) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Serving sauce of ketchup, Worcestershire, soy sauce and sugar; dashi, flour, eggs and salt batter with cabbage and scallions; spread into an even round about 2.5 cm thick in a covered skillet over medium-low 10-12 min, inverted via two plates and cooked 10 min more. (Used for the ketchup-soy-sugar sauce, without the Worcestershire, and the covered cooking and flip; the shrimp and dashi are not used.) _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_

## Own cooking judgment
- Water whisked with eggs, then flour, salt and baking powder; cabbage, spring onion and ginger folded in (NYT; SE; ATK); half the batter spread into an even round about 2.5 cm thick without pressing (NYT; ATK); covered over medium-low 8-12 min, inverted using a plate, then 8-10 min more until cooked through in the centre (all three); ketchup-soy-sugar sauce (ATK) and mayonnaise with spring onion greens (SE; NYT). No Worcestershire, dashi or pickled ginger, and the 71 °C check, are my own.
