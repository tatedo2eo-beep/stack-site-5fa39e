## Own cooking judgment
- Squash roasted until tender for deeper flavour (NYT; SE and ATK simmer or steam it)
- Onion and carrot softened in butter, simmered with the squash and stock (SE, NYT)
- Blended smooth with cream and lemon juice (SE, ATK); sage and pumpkin-seed topping is this recipe's own
