## Own cooking judgment
- Catfish steaks poached in a tamarind-water broth rather than seared
- Pineapple, okra and bạc hà (taro stems) added, with fried garlic oil on top, to match the photo
- Bean sprouts and Thai basil / rice-paddy herb in the bowl
