## Own cooking judgment
- 3 strips of almond-hazelnut meringue, 2 layers of coffee buttercream, plain top, to match the photo.
- Ganache, corn syrup and the alcohol in the sources left out; espresso dissolved in water.
- Custard base cooked to bubbling (past 85 °C).
