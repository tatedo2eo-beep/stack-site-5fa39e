## Own cooking judgment
- Soft semolina and flour dough chilled at least 2 h (NYT; ATK 24 h); onion cooked in oil then with water until soft (NYT; ATK simmers grated onion in water), passata simmered into a thick sauce and cooled (both); dough stretched in an oiled tin and risen (both); bread crumbs with grated hard cheese and oil (NYT; ATK); baked at 200 °C 30-35 min until the base is crisp (NYT); oregano and squares (both). Crumbs scattered over the sauce rather than mixed in (ATK).
