## Own cooking judgment
- Brined leaves soaked and rinsed well (both; 15 min in boiling water from NYT 1023650); rice rinsed and soaked 15 min (NYT 1023650); onion softened in olive oil with herbs (NYT 2964); stems removed, sides folded in and rolled, packed seam side down (both); plate on top, boiled then simmered until tender (NYT 1023650); lemon juice in the cooking liquid and at the end (both); served with yoghurt (NYT 1023650). The vegetarian tomato-and-three-herb filling, spices, timings and the no-salt cooking liquid are my own.
