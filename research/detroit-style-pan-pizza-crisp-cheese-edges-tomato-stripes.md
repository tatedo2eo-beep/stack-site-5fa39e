## Sources actually opened
- [Detroit-Style Pan Pizza (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/detroit-style-pizza-recipe) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Bread flour dough (about 73% water) kneaded until silky and risen 2 h; spread in an oiled anodised pan with a 30 min rest and re-stretch; sauce of garlic, oregano and crushed tomatoes simmered about 30 min until reduced; cubed brick cheese pushed to the edges, sauce in 3 rows; baked at the maximum on the lowest rack 12-15 min until the edges are black and lacy. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Detroit-Style Pizza (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024845-detroit-style-pizza) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Bread flour dough kneaded 10 min and risen 2 h; well-oiled 25 x 36 cm or 23 x 33 cm metal pan; dough stretched, rested and re-stretched until it reaches the corners, then rested 45 min; cubed brick cheese (or cheddar plus mozzarella) right to the edges and sauce spooned in three rows; baked at 260 °C on the lowest rack 15-18 min; sides loosened with a spatula. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Detroit-Style Pizza (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/9775-detroit-style-pizza) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Oiled 23 x 33 cm pan; dough pressed into the corners after a rest and risen until nearly tripled; quick sauce of crushed tomatoes, oil, garlic, herbs and sugar; cheese to the edges with stripes of sauce on top; baked on the lowest rack at 260 °C about 15 min; rested 5 min and loosened with a knife. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_

## Own cooking judgment
- Bread flour dough kneaded until silky and risen about 2 h (SE; NYT); well-oiled 23 x 33 cm metal pan with stretch, rest and re-stretch (all three); cooked garlic-oregano tomato sauce (SE; ATK quick version); cubed cheese right to the edges for the fried cheese crust, sauce in stripes on top (all three); lowest shelf at 260 °C 12-18 min (all three); sides loosened with a thin spatula (NYT; SE; ATK knife). Cheddar plus mozzarella for brick cheese (NYT).
