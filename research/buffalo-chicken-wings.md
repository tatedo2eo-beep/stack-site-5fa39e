Dish: Chicken wings / flavour: Buffalo. Private file, not published.

## Sources actually opened
- [Oven-Fried Chicken Wings (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/7583-oven-fried-chicken-wings) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Toss dried wings with baking powder + salt; wire rack; 120 °C (250 °F) 30 min on the lower-middle rack to render, then 220 °C (425 °F) 40-50 min, rotating; rest 5 min; toss in sauce. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [The Best Oven-Fried Chicken Wings (The Food Lab, Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-best-buffalo-wings-oven-fried-wings-recipe) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Baking powder (not soda, which tastes off) raises pH for browning and blistering; an uncovered overnight rest on a rack dries the skin further. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [The Best Buffalo Wings (The Food Lab, Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/ultimate-extra-crispy-double-fried-confit-buffalo-wings) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Sauce is just vinegary cayenne hot sauce + butter, melted together over medium-low; toss wings right before serving; blue cheese dressing and celery. _(opened; curl 200)_
- [Buffalo Wing Sauce Taste Test (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/what-is-the-best-buffalo-wing-sauce) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Bottle recipes tested: about 1 stick butter per cup of hot sauce (roughly 2:1 by volume); real butter beats bottled butter-flavoured sauce. _(opened; curl 200)_
- [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (USDA FSIS)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) [Safety database (locked list): USDA FSIS]: Ground meat 71.1 °C; all poultry incl. ground 73.9 °C; leftovers 73.9 °C. _(opened with WebFetch (curl gets 403 bot-block))_

## Own cooking judgment
- Butter whisked into warm hot sauce off the heat, about 2:1 by volume (Serious Eats taste test: about a stick per cup).
- Soured cream + mayo blue cheese dip.
