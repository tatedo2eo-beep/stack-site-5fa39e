## Own cooking judgment
- Chicken simmered with aromatics, shredded, broth kept; husks soaked until pliable (SE, NYT)
- Fat beaten with masa harina, baking powder, salt and warm broth until soft and light (SE, NYT)
- Masa spread on husks, filled, folded and steamed upright in a husk-lined steamer until the masa releases from the husk (SE, NYT); red chilli sauce built on the same blend-and-fry method as the green sauce
