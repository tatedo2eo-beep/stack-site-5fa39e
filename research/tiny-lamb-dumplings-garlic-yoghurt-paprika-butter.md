## Own cooking judgment
- Firm dough of flour, salt, egg and water (SE uses yolk, butter and water); lamb filling with grated onion, parsley, paprika, salt and pepper (SE); filling in the centre of a square, four corners brought up and seams pinched (NYT); boiled until tender (NYT); garlic yoghurt of yoghurt, grated garlic and salt (both); spiced tomato sauce with dried mint and Aleppo pepper (NYT), here as a butter-tomato paste sauce (my own); sumac to finish (SE). The 6-8 min simmer for homemade dough, 4 cm squares, about 80 manti, unsalted water and the 71 °C check are my own.
