## Own cooking judgment
- Marrow-bone shanks blanched, then slow-simmered in plain water (no roasting or pressure cooker)
- Corn cobs added raw; no potatoes, ginger or fried garlic
- Cabbage wedges and bok choy at the end, to match the photo
