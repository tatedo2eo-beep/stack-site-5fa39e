# Research: Slow-Cooked Dry Coconut Beef Curry with Lemongrass and Lime Leaf
Dish: Rendang / flavour: Beef (classic). Private file, not published.

## Sources actually opened
- [Beef Rendang (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/beef-rendang-recipe-8731804) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Paste of shallots, red chillies, galangal, ginger, garlic, candlenuts, coriander seed, turmeric and salt blitzed 2-3 min; combined with two cans coconut milk, makrut lime leaves, knotted lemongrass, cinnamon and tamarind and simmered; chuck added; cooked uncovered in a 160 C oven about 2 h, stirring every 30 min, until reduced with oil on top; finished on the hob 45 min-1 h, scraping often, until dark and clinging. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Rendang Daging (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1021518-rendang-daging-beef-rendang) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Paste of chillies, shallots, garlic, ginger, galangal, turmeric, coriander and cumin; beef simmered with the paste, coconut milk, lemongrass, lime and bay leaves 2-2.5 h, stirring every 20 min, until the oil splits; then reduced over higher heat about 15 min, stirring continuously, until deep brown. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Beef Rendang (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/16133-beef-rendang) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Paste with ginger, galangal, lemongrass, chillies, lime leaves, coriander, cumin and turmeric cooked with half a can of coconut milk 8-12 min; remaining coconut milk and chuck added and cooked uncovered in the oven about 2-2.25 h; then cooked on the hob 10-15 min until the liquid has evaporated and meat sizzles in the fat, plus about 5 min until deep brown. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
