## Own cooking judgment
- Dried guajillo and árbol chillies softened (toasted first in NYT) and blended with tomatoes, garlic and spices including cumin, cloves, cinnamon, oregano and pepper (all three)
- Chuck braised in the chilli purée with water, onion and bay without searing until fork-tender (SE 3-4 h; ATK 2.5 h; NYT oven 2 h)
- Rested, fat skimmed and kept; tortillas dragged through the fat, filled with mozzarella and shredded beef, folded and crisped, served with broth for dipping (ATK)
