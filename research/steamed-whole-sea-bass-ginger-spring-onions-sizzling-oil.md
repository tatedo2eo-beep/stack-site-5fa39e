## Own cooking judgment
- Shallow slashes on both sides (ATK 16301); salt and ginger in the cavity (ATK; SE); fish raised on spring onion and ginger on the plate (ATK; SE); foil sling into a covered wok over boiling water (ATK); steamed 10-12 min until opaque with no translucence at the bone (ATK; NYT 1015025; SE about 10 min); steaming juices poured off (SE); spring-onion greens, ginger and chilli on top and smoking oil poured over (ATK); coriander (NYT; SE); top fillet lifted, then backbone removed (NYT). My own: a simple soy, sugar and water sauce in place of rice wine; doneness to the site minimum of 63 °C.
