## Sources actually opened
- [Nashville Hot Chicken Sandwich (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/nashville-hot-chicken-sandwich-recipe-8403635) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Buttermilk + hot sauce brine; rest dredged chicken ~15 min so flour hydrates; oil ~175 °C; thighs to 80 °C; cayenne/brown sugar bloomed in hot oil and basted on immediately. _(read via search extract; curl got 403 bot-block)_
- [5-Ingredient Fried Chicken Sandwiches (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/five-ingredient-fried-chicken-sandwich-recipe) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Boneless thighs; buttermilk dip; drizzle ~3 tbsp buttermilk into the flour for shaggy clumps, press on firmly; hold oil 150-177 °C after the drop. _(opened; curl 200)_
- [Nashville Hot Smash Burger (Grillfürst)](https://www.grillfuerst.de/magazin/grillrezepte/nashville-hot-smash-burger/) [NOT on the locked list: context only, not relied on for method, ratios, temperatures or flavours]: Cayenne/brown sugar/paprika/garlic paste with hot oil; brush AFTER it leaves the heat or cayenne burns bitter; pickles + slaw cut heat. _(read via search extract; URL verified 200)_
- [Spicy Nashville Smash Burger (Culinary Aura)](https://www.culinaryaura.com/spicy-nashville-smash-burger/) [NOT on the locked list: context only, not relied on for method, ratios, temperatures or flavours]: Same spice set brushed on after cooking. _(read via search extract; URL verified 200)_
- [Buffalo Chicken Sandwich (FOX13, chef recipe)](https://www.fox13now.com/the-place/recipes/foodie-friday-cougar-tail-buffalo-chicken-sandwich) [NOT on the locked list: context only, not relied on for method, ratios, temperatures or flavours]: Hot sauce in the buttermilk; drizzle buttermilk into the dredge for craggy crust; fry at 175 °C to 74 °C; sauce warmed not boiled. _(read via search extract)_
- [Fried Chicken Sandwiches (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/7723-fried-chicken-sandwiches) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Clumpy baking-powder dredge (liquid rubbed into the flour), coated pieces rested on a rack, fried with the oil held at 163-177 °C until cooked through and drained on a rack, then served on soft rolls with pickles. This supports our crust and frying method. _(curl 200; read 2026-10-22)_
- [Nashville Hot Fried Chicken (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/6009-nashville-hot-fried-chicken) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Hot-sauce brine; a spicy oil of 3 tbsp oil heated until shimmering, with 1 tbsp cayenne, paprika, garlic powder and sugar cooked in for 30 seconds, then brushed over the fried chicken. This matches our warm-oil cayenne glaze; ours adds the spices off the heat so they can't scorch. _(curl 200; read 2026-10-22)_

## Own cooking judgment
- 2026-10-22 second-source check (ATK): the crust, frying temperatures and spiced-oil glaze all agree. No changes. Salt is 3.04 g a serving, which is 'about 3 g' (noted for Joe).
- Fresh oil warmed to about 120 °C and taken off the heat before the spices go in, instead of ladling hot frying oil (both top-tier sources ladle frying oil; a small pan of fresh oil is easier and safer at home and still blooms the cayenne).
- Slaw quantities.
