## Own cooking judgment
- Ground spice paste of coriander, cumin, fennel, chilli and turmeric (SE fish head curry; SE tamarind curry blooms the same spices); aubergine browned first; mustard and fenugreek seeds tempered; onion, garlic, ginger and tomato; curry leaves and tamarind water (both); split fish heads simmered covered with aubergine, okra and green chillies (SE fish head curry) to at least 63 °C by site rule; sugar and coconut milk to finish.
