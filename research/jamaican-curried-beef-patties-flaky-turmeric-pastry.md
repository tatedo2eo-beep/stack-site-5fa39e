## Own cooking judgment
- All-butter pastry (no shortening or suet) and no Worcestershire or soy sauce.
- Breadcrumbs and water as the binder, mince cooked to 71 °C and the baked filling checked at 74 °C.
- 8 patties of about 70 g filling each so salt stays near 1.3 g per patty.
