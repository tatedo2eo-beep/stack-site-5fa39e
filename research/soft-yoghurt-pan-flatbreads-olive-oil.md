## Own cooking judgment
- Yoghurt and olive oil in the dough for a tender, bendable bread.
- Cooked in a dry, lightly oiled pan at about 220 °C until freckled and puffed.
- Stacked in a towel to stay soft; 8 breads with 1 1/4 tsp salt.
