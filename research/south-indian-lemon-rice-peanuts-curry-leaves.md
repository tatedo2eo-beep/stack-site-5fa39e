## Own cooking judgment
- Basmati rinsed, soaked 20 min and cooked with salt and turmeric, then rested 10 min (ATK); peanuts fried in coconut oil until crisp (ATK; SE); chana dal, urad dal and mustard seeds fried until the dals turn light brown (SE; ATK); asafoetida and turmeric (ATK; SE); curry leaves fried until crisp (ATK; SE); green chillies (ATK; SE); lemon or lime juice (ATK; SE) and rice folded in gently (SE). Dried red chillies, 1.5 ml water per g rice and less lemon juice are my own.
