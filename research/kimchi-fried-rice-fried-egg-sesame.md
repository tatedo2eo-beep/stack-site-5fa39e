## Own cooking judgment
- Onion and scallion whites softened (ATK; NYT); kimchi and its juice cooked until soft and reduced (NYT; ATK; SE); butter (SE; NYT); cold day-old rice folded in until evenly coated (all three); soy sauce (NYT; ATK) and sesame oil (all three); rice left to crisp on the base (SE; NYT; ATK); fried eggs cooked under a lid (SE); sesame seeds and scallion greens (ATK; NYT). Chilli flakes instead of chilli paste, no meat, eggs cooked until the yolks are fully set and the 71/74 °C checks are my own.
