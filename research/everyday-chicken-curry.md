- [Chicken Curry with Cardamom (Madhur Jaffrey, from Curry Easy)](https://thehappyfoodie.co.uk/recipes/chicken-curry-with-cardamom/) [Tier 1 addition (locked list): Madhur Jaffrey, recipe from her book on the publisher's site]: Brown onions, add garlic, then ground coriander, cumin, turmeric and cayenne; tomatoes; simmer the chicken in the sauce. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
