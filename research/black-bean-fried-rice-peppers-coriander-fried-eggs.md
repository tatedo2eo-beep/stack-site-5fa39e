## Own cooking judgment
- Rice cooked ahead and chilled (ATK cold cooked rice; SE refrigerated 24 h)
- Onion, garlic and peppers sautéed, beans added with a little liquid (ATK, SE)
- Cold rice folded in and fried until coated and hot, coriander added off the heat; served with eggs (ATK, SE)
