## Own cooking judgment
- Onions cooked covered with garlic and thyme about 20 min, then uncovered at medium-low about 25 min until very soft and pale golden, with water if they catch, and cooled (NYT; SE; ATK); 250 g flour yeasted olive-oil dough risen and pressed into an oiled tray (NYT; SE); anchovy lattice with Niçoise olives (all three); baked until the crust is golden (all three); thyme to finish (SE). Anchovies rinsed (ATK) and kept as whole fillets, with less onion salt (my own).
