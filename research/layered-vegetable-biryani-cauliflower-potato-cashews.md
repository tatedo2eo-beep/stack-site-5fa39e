## Own cooking judgment
- Onions caramelised and half reserved, with cashews and raisins fried for the top (SE); garlic, ginger, chilli and spices fried, then yoghurt, cauliflower, potato, carrot and water simmered covered until tender, peas added (SE; NYT also green beans); rice rinsed, soaked and parboiled with spices and drained (SE); layered and topped with saffron milk and the garnish, sealed with foil and lid (SE); rested before serving (SE; NYT; ATK); herbs (all three). Layering over the vegetables in one pot, oven steaming as for the lamb version, no apricots or rosewater and the 74 °C check are my own.
