## Own cooking judgment
- Paste of soaked dried chillies, coriander stems, shallots, turmeric, lemongrass, ginger, garlic, curry powder and lime zest blended smooth (NYT Khao Soi Gai; SE pounds similar aromatics); part of the noodles fried crisp (all three); coconut cream cracked and paste fried, then coconut milk and stock (all three); drumsticks simmered covered 20-30 min (NYT; SE 30 min); fish sauce and palm sugar (all); served over boiled noodles with shallot and lime (all). Omitting shrimp paste and pickled mustard greens, the 160 C oil and the 74 C target are my own.
