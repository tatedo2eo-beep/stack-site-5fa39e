## Own cooking judgment
- Very wet bread flour dough with oil; salt added after a 10 min rest and strengthened by folds with 20 min rests (ATK); refrigerated 16-24 h (ATK); pressed into a well-oiled tray with oiled fingers and risen until puffy (both); blended or chopped tomatoes with oil and salt spread thinly (both); lowest shelf at 230-250 °C 20-30 min (both); mozzarella in the last 5 min (NYT); basil (NYT); cut into rectangles (ATK).
