## Own cooking judgment
- Onion and long green peppers softened in olive oil with chilli flakes or paprika and oregano (ATK, NYT, SE)
- Tomatoes cooked until soft and most moisture evaporated (ATK, NYT, SE)
- Beaten eggs stirred gently over low heat until set; parsley and bread to serve (ATK, NYT, SE)
