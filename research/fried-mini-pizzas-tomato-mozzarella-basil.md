## Own cooking judgment
- Yeasted dough with olive oil risen about 1.5 h and divided (both); rounds pressed out on a floured surface (ATK); fried in about 4 cm of oil at 175-180 °C, pressed or basted to puff and turned once, then drained on racks (ATK; NYT 175 °C); warm thick tomato sauce, mozzarella and grated hard cheese (both); melted under the grill (ATK); basil (ATK).
