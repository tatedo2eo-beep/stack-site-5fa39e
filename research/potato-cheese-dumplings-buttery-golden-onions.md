## Own cooking judgment
- Soft dough of flour, salt, egg, sour cream and soft butter worked with the fingertips and rested 30 min (KAB; SE); potatoes simmered until tender, riced hot and mixed with farmer's cheese and cheddar, cooled before filling (ATK; SE; NYT); onions cooked in butter 15-20 min until deep golden (ATK; SE), part into the filling and part kept to serve (NYT); rounds cut, filled, folded and pinched/fork-sealed (KAB; SE); boiled in batches until they float and are tender (all); pan-fried in butter with onions (KAB; ATK; NYT); sour cream (all) and chives (SE). The 2 mm roll, 7.5 cm rounds, unsalted water and the 74 °C reheat are my own.
