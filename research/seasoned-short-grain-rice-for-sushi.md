## Own cooking judgment
- Short-grain rice rinsed until the water runs almost clear (all three); about 1.2 ml water per g rice (SE; NYT), here soaked 30 min first (my own); covered, low heat, then rested off the heat (ATK 10 min; NYT about 15 min); rice vinegar, sugar and salt warmed to dissolve (NYT) and folded into the hot rice with a paddle while fanning until glossy (NYT; SE). The 12 min cook, less salt, no kombu and the 2-hour holding limit are my own.
