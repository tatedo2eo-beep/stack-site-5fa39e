## Own cooking judgment
- Paste fried in coconut oil and coconut milk until it spits, then cracked with coconut milk added in increments until oil separates (SE; NYT cooks paste in coconut cream 3 min; NYT panang breaks the cream first); fish sauce and palm sugar (SE); chicken thigh with lime leaves and thinned coconut milk about 10 min (SE); aubergine 3 min and basil to finish (SE). The smaller amount of paste, sliced lime leaf garnish and 74 C target are my own.
