## Own cooking judgment
- Basmati washed until clear and soaked (all three); parboiled 5-7 min until lengthened but firm in the centre, drained and rinsed cold (all three); ground saffron steeped in hot water (SE; NYT 1020207); a yoghurt rice crust layer in hot butter and oil (NYT 1018646); rice mounded away from the sides with 6-8 holes (SE; NYT 1018646); uncovered sizzle 8 min (NYT 1018646); butter-water drizzled over (SE); towel-wrapped lid and 45-50 min on low with quarter turns (all three); pot cooled briefly to release the crust (SE) and flipped onto a platter (NYT 1020207; NYT 1018646). The egg yolk in the crust, the 71 °C check, unsalted soak, much less parboiling salt and the barberry topping are my own.
