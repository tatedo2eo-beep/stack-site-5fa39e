## Own cooking judgment
- Lamb marinated overnight in yoghurt, garlic, ginger and salt (SE; NYT); onions browned in ghee or oil and half reserved (SE; NYT); whole and ground spices fried briefly (SE; NYT); lamb simmered with marinade, water, coriander, mint and chilli until tender, then reduced (SE); rice rinsed, soaked and parboiled 2-3 min with lemon, salt and cardamom, drained (SE; NYT 3 min); rice spread over the lamb, saffron milk and fried onions on top (SE; NYT), butter (NYT); sealed with foil and lid and baked at 180 °C (SE; NYT); rested (SE; NYT). The 25 min bake, 50-60 min simmer for 3 cm cubes, no rosewater or kewra and the 74 °C check are my own.
