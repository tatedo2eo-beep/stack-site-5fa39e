## Own cooking judgment
- 300 g bread flour, sugar, salt, 1 g instant yeast, cold water and oil, rested 10 min and mixed until silky (NYT); cold-fermented (NYT; SE); rolled very thin on semolina and dried until leathery (NYT); smooth blended sauce (SE); finely grated Parmesan and low-moisture mozzarella to the edges (SE); baked on a very hot stone or steel until crisp and cut into squares (SE; NYT). Salt halved, and fennel seed, green pepper and onion in place of sausage, are my own.
