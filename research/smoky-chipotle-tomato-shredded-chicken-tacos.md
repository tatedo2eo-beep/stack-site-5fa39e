## Own cooking judgment
- Thighs browned, then onion, garlic and tomatillo browned in the fat with oregano and cumin (SE; ATK browns boneless thighs in batches)
- Tomatoes, chipotles and adobo blended smooth (SE, ATK Shredded); chicken simmered in the sauce to 74 °C (SE) about 20-25 min (ATK)
- Shredded chicken cooked in the sauce until it clings, 8-15 min (both ATK); served with onion, coriander, fresh cheese and lime (SE, NYT)
