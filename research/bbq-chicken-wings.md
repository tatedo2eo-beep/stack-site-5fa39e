## Sources actually opened
- [Oven-Fried Chicken Wings (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/7583-oven-fried-chicken-wings) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Toss dried wings with baking powder + salt; wire rack; 120 °C (250 °F) 30 min on the lower-middle rack to render, then 220 °C (425 °F) 40-50 min, rotating; rest 5 min; toss in sauce. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Oven-Fried Barbecue Chicken Wings (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/14546-toaster-oven-oven-fried-barbecue-chicken-wings) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Bake wings crisp first, rest 5 min, then toss in a ketchup-molasses-cider-vinegar barbecue sauce (no brushing in the oven). _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [The Best Oven-Fried Chicken Wings (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-best-buffalo-wings-oven-fried-wings-recipe) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Wings patted dry, tossed with baking powder and salt (1 tsp of each per 450 g), set spaced out on a rack over a lined tray and left uncovered in the fridge for 8-24 hours. Roasted on the upper-middle shelf at 230 °C, turned, until crisp and golden (35-50 minutes total), then tossed in a warm sauce in a bowl just before serving. This matches our baking powder, rack, dry skin and toss-after-baking method. _(curl 200; read 2026-10-22)_
- [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (USDA FSIS)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) [Safety database (locked list): USDA FSIS]: Ground meat 71.1 °C; all poultry incl. ground 73.9 °C; leftovers 73.9 °C. _(opened with WebFetch (curl gets 403 bot-block))_
