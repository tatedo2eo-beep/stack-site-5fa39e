## Own cooking judgment
- Coconut cream and yellow curry paste cooked until the fat splits (SE; ATK fries paste until browning); potatoes, onion wedges, coconut milk and fish sauce with just enough water to cover, simmered about 15 min (SE; ATK 12-14 min); chicken simmered covered until cooked (SE 5-8 min; ATK 10-12 min). Thighs only, the 8-10 min timing, omitting the relish and the 74 C target are my own.
