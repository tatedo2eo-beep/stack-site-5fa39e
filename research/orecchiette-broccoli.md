## Own cooking judgment
- Semolina dough at 50 % water; orecchiette dragged with a knife and inverted, cavatelli option.
- Broccoli and pasta cooked in the same pot; garlic-chilli oil; finished with cooking water (04-P3, 04-P4).
- Anchovy left out so it stays vegetarian.
