# Research: Creamy Cashew and Yoghurt Chicken Curry with Cardamom
Dish: Korma / flavour: Chicken. Private file, not published.

## Sources actually opened
- [Amu's Chicken Korma (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1021424-amus-chicken-korma) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Chicken coated in a puree of ginger, garlic, yoghurt and salt; sliced onions fried crisp in ghee; onion puree browned with coriander, bay, cinnamon and cardamom 10-12 min; chicken and marinade covered 10 min then cooked 30-35 min until thick and the fat breaks out. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Golden Chicken Korma (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/17367-golden-chicken-korma) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Cashews blended with hot water and soaked 15 min; onions browned 10-15 min and blended smooth with the cashews and a tomato; whole cloves, cinnamon and bay bloomed 30-60 s, then garlic, ginger and ground spices; sauce simmered covered 10 min; chicken simmered covered 10-15 min; finished with coriander and chopped cashews. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Cashew Butter Chicken Korma (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1026005-cashew-butter-chicken-korma) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Onion, ginger, garlic and cumin cooked in ghee about 5 min; boneless thigh cooked about 15 min; chilli powder, turmeric and tomatoes 5-7 min; finished with yoghurt and cashew butter and an optional drizzle of cream. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (USDA FSIS)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) [Safety database (locked list): USDA FSIS]: Ground meat 71.1 °C; all poultry incl. ground 73.9 °C; leftovers 73.9 °C. _(opened with WebFetch (curl gets 403 bot-block))_
