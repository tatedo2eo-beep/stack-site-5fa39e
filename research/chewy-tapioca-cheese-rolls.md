- [Cuñapes/Pão de Queijo (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/south-american-cheesy-bread-recipe) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Sour tapioca starch, salt, baking powder, milk, melted butter and eggs kneaded about 2 min, two cheeses worked in, chilled overnight to hydrate the starch; rolled into 5 cm balls and baked at 190 °C about 20 min until puffed and golden; can be frozen and baked from frozen. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Brazilian Cheese Buns (Pão de Queijo) (King Arthur Baking)](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/brazilian-cheese-buns-pao-de-queijo-recipe) [Tier 1 (locked list): King Arthur Baking]: Butter, water, milk and salt brought to a full boil and beaten into tapioca starch until smooth and elastic; dough cooled until no longer uncomfortably hot before eggs are dribbled in; cheese beaten in; scooped into golf-ball portions and baked at 190 °C. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_

## Own cooking judgment
- Boiling milk, oil and butter mixed into tapioca starch (ATK, KA, NYT); paste cooled until warm before the eggs (KA, NYT)
- Eggs beaten in until shiny, sticky and clinging to the bowl (ATK); cheeses mixed in; dough chilled at least 1-2 h or overnight (ATK, NYT, SE)
- Shaped with damp hands, egg-washed, started at 230 °C and baked at 190 °C (ATK); can be frozen and baked from frozen (SE)
