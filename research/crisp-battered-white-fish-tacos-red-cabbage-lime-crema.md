## Own cooking judgment
- Firm white fish cut into fingers (SE, NYT); light batter of flour, egg and sparkling water mixed just before frying (SE); deep-fried at 190 °C (SE); fish cooked until golden, 3-4 min (NYT)
- Mayonnaise and soured cream sauce seasoned with lime (NYT; SE uses mayonnaise-crema slaw); shredded cabbage (NYT, SE)
- Toppings of lime and coriander (all); grilled method of ATK not used
