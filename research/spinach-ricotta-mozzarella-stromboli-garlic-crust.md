## Own cooking judgment
- Dough rolled to 30 x 40 cm (NYT); greens cooked briefly with garlic and squeezed dry in a towel (ATK); cheeses spread with borders, sides folded in and rolled tightly, seam brushed with egg and sealed, seam-down on the tray (both); egg glaze, Parmesan, oregano and slashes (both); baked at about 200 °C 25-35 min until golden and the centre is hot (NYT; ATK 93 °C); rested 10-15 min before slicing; tomato sauce for dipping (NYT). Ricotta and nutmeg in the spinach filling are my own, to match the photo.
