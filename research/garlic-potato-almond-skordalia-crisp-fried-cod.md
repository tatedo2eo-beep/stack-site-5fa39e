## Own cooking judgment
- Lemon juice only as the acid (no vinegar).
- Served with crisp sparkling-water-battered cod fingers and lemon, as in the photo; fish fried at 180 °C to 63 °C.
- Potato blended by hand, never in the processor, to avoid a gluey texture.
