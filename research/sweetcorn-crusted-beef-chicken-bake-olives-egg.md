## Own cooking judgment
- Corn blended or grated with dairy and cooked until thick, basil added (ATK, SE)
- Onions cooked with cumin and paprika, beef browned and thickened (SE with flour and reserved broth); chicken poached or steamed and shredded (ATK, SE, NYT for the beef)
- Beef layered with egg, olives, raisins and chicken, topped with corn, sprinkled with sugar and baked until golden (ATK, SE, NYT)
