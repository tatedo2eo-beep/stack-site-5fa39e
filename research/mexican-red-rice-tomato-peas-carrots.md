## Sources actually opened
- [Arroz Rojo, Mexican Red Rice (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/arroz-rojo-mexican-red-rice-recipe-11723710) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Long-grain rice rinsed and dried at least 5 min; onion softened 3-5 min and garlic 30 s, lifted into a blender with stock, tomatoes and salt and blended smooth; rice toasted in oil 5-8 min until golden; tomato mixture simmered 3-5 min; rest of the stock, pricked serrano, peas and carrots scattered on top; covered on medium-low 12-18 min; rested covered 10-20 min and fluffed. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Mexican Rice (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1016674-mexican-rice) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Garlic, tomatoes, green pepper, onion, jalapeño, salt and stock blended into a mostly smooth purée; rinsed rice toasted in oil until nutty; purée added and brought to a light boil; covered on the lowest heat 17 min undisturbed and rested 10 min. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Arroz Rojo, Red Rice (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1023392-arroz-rojo-red-rice) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Warm chicken stock mixed with tomato paste, onion powder, coriander, cumin and pepper; garlic and rice toasted in oil 3-4 min; stock mixture boiled, mixed vegetables stirred in, covered on low 15-20 min; steamed off the heat 15 min, uncovered 5 min and fluffed; salt to taste. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
