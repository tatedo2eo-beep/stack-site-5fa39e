## Own cooking judgment
- Rice, beans and spiced onion cooked in chipotle broth with chicken covered on top until cooked through, then diced and mixed with cheddar and coriander (ATK Chimichangas; ATK Baked heats a similar filling)
- Tortillas warmed until pliable, filled, folded and sealed with flour paste (ATK; NYT and ATK Baked fold and roll tightly)
- Fried seam side down about 2 min per side at 150-165 °C (ATK; NYT shallow-fries at 175 °C); served with soured cream, pico de gallo and guacamole (NYT)
