# Research: Musakhan (Sumac Chicken with Onions on Flatbread)
Dish: Musakhan / flavour: Classic. Private file, not published.

## Sources actually opened
- [Chicken Musakhan (Ottolenghi / Sami Tamimi)](https://ottolenghi.co.uk/pages/recipes/chicken-musakhan) [Tier 1 (locked list): Ottolenghi]: Sumac, cumin, cinnamon, allspice on the chicken; red onions cooked in plenty of olive oil about 15 min until soft and pale golden, NOT caramelised, then coated in sumac; pine nuts toasted; flatbread crisped under the grill; assembled with the roasting juices. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Mussakhan (NYT Cooking, chef recipe)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1019936-mussakhan-roast-chicken-with-sumac-and-red-onions) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Marinate bone-in chicken with lemon, olive oil, sumac, garlic, cumin, allspice, cinnamon and red onion 1-3 h; roast at 190 °C about 40 min; toast pine nuts; assemble on warm taboon with onions, juices, parsley and sumac. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Msakhan (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/msakhan-recipe-5219618) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Jammy sumac-spiced onions on flatbreads crisped under heat, topped with spiced roast chicken. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Musakhan (Bon Appétit)](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/musakhan) [NOT on the locked list: context only, not relied on for method, ratios, temperatures or flavours]: Chicken browned, onions slow-cooked in plenty of olive oil with sumac, then piled onto flatbread. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (USDA FSIS)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) [Safety database (locked list): USDA FSIS]: Ground meat 71.1 °C; all poultry incl. ground 73.9 °C; leftovers 73.9 °C. _(opened with WebFetch (curl gets 403 bot-block))_
- [USDA FoodData Central (API, SR Legacy)](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/) [Nutrition database (locked list): USDA FoodData Central]: Per-100 g nutrient values for every ingredient (ids in the nutrition note). _(queried via API)_
