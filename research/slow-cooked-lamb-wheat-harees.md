## Own cooking judgment
- Meat and wheat simmered together for a long time, stirring more towards the end, then mashed hard (SE harissa); beaten until stretchy with fine meat strands (SE haleem); whole spices in the pot and spiced ghee pool on top are my Gulf-style choices to match the photo.
