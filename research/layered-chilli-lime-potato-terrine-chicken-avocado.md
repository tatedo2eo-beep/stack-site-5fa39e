## Own cooking judgment
- Potatoes cooked, riced while warm and seasoned with ají amarillo paste, lime, oil and salt (SE, NYT)
- Mayonnaise-bound chicken (or tuna/shrimp) and avocado layered between potato in a dish or ring moulds and pressed lightly (SE, NYT)
- Chilled before serving and garnished with olives and herbs (SE, NYT)
