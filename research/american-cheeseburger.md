# Research: Diner-Style American Cheeseburger
Dish: Cheeseburger / flavour: American. Private file, not published.

## Sources actually opened
- [Salting Ground Beef (The Burger Lab, Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-burger-lab-salting-ground-beef) [Top-tier: test kitchen / food science]: Salt mixed into mince early gives a springy, sausage-like texture; salt the outside just before cooking. _(read via search extract)_
- [When to Salt Burgers (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/how_tos/6586-when-to-salt-burgers) [Top-tier: test kitchen]: Confirms: season the surface immediately before cooking (myosin gel explanation). _(read via search extract)_
- [This 2-Second Burger Trick Prevents That Annoying Dome in the Middle (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/flat-burger-cooking-prep-tip-12128439) [Top-tier: test kitchen / food science]: Shape patty ~1 cm wider than bun; shallow 6 mm dimple; do not press with spatula. _(read via search extract)_
- [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (USDA FSIS)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) [Top-tier: food safety (government)]: Ground meat 71.1 °C; all poultry incl. ground 73.9 °C; leftovers 73.9 °C. _(opened with WebFetch (curl gets 403 bot-block))_
- [USDA FoodData Central (API, SR Legacy)](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/) [Top-tier: nutrition data (government)]: Per-100 g nutrient values for every ingredient (ids in the nutrition note). _(queried via API)_
