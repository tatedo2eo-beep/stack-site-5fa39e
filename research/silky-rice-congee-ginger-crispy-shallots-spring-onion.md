## Own cooking judgment
- Rice rinsed (ATK; NYT); simmered with a little stock and plenty of water (ATK; NYT; SE all stock), with ginger (SE; NYT), stirring often, lid ajar on a wooden spoon (ATK), until thick, glossy and creamy (all three), adjusting with water (SE; NYT); shallots fried in oil until crisp (SE); spring onions and white pepper (SE), soy sauce (ATK; NYT) and sesame oil (NYT) to serve. The 1:13 rice to liquid ratio, 60-75 min, mixed stock and water to hold salt down, and cold-start shallots are my own.
