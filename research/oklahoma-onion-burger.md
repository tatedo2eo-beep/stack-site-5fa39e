## Sources actually opened
- [Oklahoma-Style Onion Burgers Recipe (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/oklahoma-onion-burger-recipe): Paper-thin onion pressed into the patty during the smash; onion cooks underneath after flip. _(opened)_
- [Fried onion burger (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fried_onion_burger): History (El Reno, 1920s); buns steamed on the patty; yellow mustard and pickles; American cheese common. _(opened)_
- [Classic Smashed Burgers Recipe (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/classic-smashed-burgers-recipe): Smash once, early, on a very hot pan; scrape under the crust to flip. _(opened)_
- [The Ultimate Smash Burger: Griddle Temps for the Perfect Crust (ThermoWorks)](https://www.thermoworks.com/blogs/thermoblog/smash-burgers-at-home): Surface 232 °C+ for crust; pull at 71 °C; thermometer works even on thin patties. _(opened)_
- [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (USDA FSIS)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart): Ground meat 71.1 °C; all poultry incl. ground 73.9 °C; leftovers 73.9 °C. _(opened with WebFetch (curl gets 403 bot-block))_

## Own cooking judgment
- 35-40 g onion per ball; salting onions first; toasting the bottom bun but steaming the top (compromise between tradition and Joe's toasted-bun rule).
