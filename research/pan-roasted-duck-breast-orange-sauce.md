## Own cooking judgment
- Scored skin, cold-to-gentle pan render 15-20 min until crisp (ATK 5715, NYT 1013774); pan sauce with shallot, broth and butter (ATK 5715); orange-lime-honey reduction (ATK 2483) with zest and segments to match the photo; no liqueur or wine; finished in the oven to at least 74 °C by the poultry rule.
