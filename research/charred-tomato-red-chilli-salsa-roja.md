## Own cooking judgment
- Tomatoes, garlic, onion and a fresh red chilli blackened together in a dry pan, no foil
- Whole toasted árbol chillies crumbled into the molcajete instead of a separate chilli powder
- Lime wedges and an extra charred chilli to serve, to match the photo
