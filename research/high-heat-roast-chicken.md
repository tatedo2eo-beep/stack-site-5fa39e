## Own cooking judgment
- 230 °C (210 °C fan) for 50-60 min for 1.6 kg, from the NYT Cooking method; the fallback to 200 °C if the oven smokes is from the same recipe.
- Pan sauce of butter and mustard; thigh 77-80 °C for tenderness (USDA minimum 74 °C).
