## Sources actually opened
- [Oven-Fried Chicken Wings (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/7583-oven-fried-chicken-wings) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Toss dried wings with baking powder + salt; wire rack; 120 °C (250 °F) 30 min on the lower-middle rack to render, then 220 °C (425 °F) 40-50 min, rotating; rest 5 min; toss in sauce. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [The Best Oven-Fried Chicken Wings (The Food Lab, Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-best-buffalo-wings-oven-fried-wings-recipe) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Baking powder (not soda, which tastes off) raises pH for browning and blistering; an uncovered overnight rest on a rack dries the skin further. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Baking Powder: The Secret Weapon for Crispy Chicken Skin (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/articles/3717-baking-powder-the-secret-weapon-for-crispy-chicken-skin) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Baking powder helps dry the skin, breaks down skin proteins and aids browning. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
