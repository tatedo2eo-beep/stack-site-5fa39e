## Own cooking judgment
- Bone-in lamb shoulder trimmed, slit all over and salted (NYT 1016562); spice butter of toasted ground cumin and coriander, paprika, smoked paprika and garlic (NYT 1016562); spice rub then a 1 h rest out of the fridge (NYT 1017718; NYT 1016562); roasted hot 30 min then slowly 3.5-4 h with basting, tented if browning, until the meat pulls from the bone (NYT 1016562; NYT 1017718 cooks until nearly falling apart); torn by hand and served with cumin salt (both). My own: water in the tin, warm flatbread and lemon to match the photo, and a 15 min rest.
