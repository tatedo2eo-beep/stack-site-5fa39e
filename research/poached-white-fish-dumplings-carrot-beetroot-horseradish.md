## Own cooking judgment
- Vegetable poaching broth with peppercorns (NYT 1018051; SE stock vegetables); onion and carrot pulsed, then fish pulsed until chopped but not mushy (NYT 1018051); eggs, oil, matzo meal, salt, sugar, pepper and cold water mixed until slightly sticky (SE; NYT 1018051); a small test ball cooked to check the seasoning (NYT 6674); ovals shaped with wet hands (NYT 1018051); poached covered until firm with carrots, carrot slices on top (NYT 1018051); served chilled or warm with horseradish (NYT 1018051; NYT 5131). Beetroot in the horseradish is my own (common in Ashkenazi kitchens); the cook-to-71 °C check reflects the site egg rule. Poaching time lengthened slightly for 90 g dumplings.
