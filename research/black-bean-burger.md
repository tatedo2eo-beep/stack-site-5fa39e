Dish: Veggie/bean burger / flavour: Classic. Private file, not published.

## Sources actually opened
- [Really Awesome Black Bean Burgers (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-best-black-bean-burger-recipe) [Top-tier: test kitchen / food science]: Bake drained tinned beans ~20 min at 175 °C to dry them: dense, meaty, not mushy. _(read via search extract)_
- [Black Bean Burgers (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/8509-black-bean-burgers) [Top-tier: test kitchen]: Dry rinsed beans thoroughly; pulse coarsely so beans keep texture; egg + flour binder; chill mix 1 h; pan-fry over medium ~5 min a side until well browned. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Black Bean Smash Burgers (Simply Recipes)](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/black-bean-smash-burgers-recipe-6979384) [Context only (not top-tier; not relied on for method, ratios or temperatures)]: Drying beans fixes wetness; flip gently with a wide spatula. _(read via search extract; curl from box got 403 bot-block, page returned by search)_
- [The Best Black Bean Burgers (Sally's Baking)](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/best-black-bean-burgers/) [Context only (not top-tier; not relied on for method, ratios or temperatures)]: Bake beans ~15 min; sauté onion/pepper/garlic and blot; leave chunks of bean for texture; cumin + smoked paprika. _(read via search extract; curl from box got 403 bot-block, page returned by search)_
- [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (USDA FSIS)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) [Top-tier: food safety (government)]: Ground meat 71.1 °C; all poultry incl. ground 73.9 °C; leftovers 73.9 °C. _(opened with WebFetch (curl gets 403 bot-block))_
- [USDA FoodData Central (API, SR Legacy)](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/) [Top-tier: nutrition data (government)]: Per-100 g nutrient values for every ingredient (ids in the nutrition note). _(queried via API)_

> Re-checked on 22 Oct after the source-quality rule: drying the beans, pulsing coarsely, chilling and pan-frying over medium are backed by Serious Eats and America's Test Kitchen. Oven-drying at 180 °C (160 °C fan) for 15-20 min follows Serious Eats (about 175 °C, about 20 min). Simply Recipes and Sally's Baking are context only.
