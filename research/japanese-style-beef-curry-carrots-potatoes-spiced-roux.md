## Sources actually opened
- [Kare Raisu (Homemade Japanese Curry Rice) (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/japanese-curry-kare) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Homemade spice blend of coriander, cumin, fenugreek, cardamom, pepper, fennel, cinnamon, cloves, star anise, turmeric and chilli; chicken browned about 6 min; onion cooked golden about 10 min, carrots 4 min; stock and dashi simmered; potatoes and grated apple simmered about 15 min; butter-flour roux cooked to deep caramel about 20 min, ginger and spice blend stirred in 1 min, then stirred into the stew and simmered until thick. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Kare Raisu (Japanese Curry Rice with Chicken) (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/15953-kare-raisu-japanese-curry-rice-with-chicken) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Onion browned about 8 min with ginger and garlic; chicken, potatoes and carrots simmered in broth about 20 min until just tender; curry-roux brick, soy sauce and Worcestershire dissolved and simmered about 3 min until thick; served beside short-grain rice. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Japanese Curry-Roux Bricks (America's Test Kitchen)](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/recipes/15954-japanese-curry-roux-bricks) [Tier 1 (locked list): America's Test Kitchen]: Ground spice mix with turmeric, coriander, ginger, cardamom, cumin, cinnamon, fennel, fenugreek, garlic powder, mustard and pepper; butter and flour cooked to pale golden 3-4 min; spices whisked in off the heat, then miso; chilled until set. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_
- [Japanese Curry Brick (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020451-japanese-curry-brick) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Whole spices toasted about 2 min and ground with dried shiitake and kombu; turmeric, ginger, salt, paprika and cayenne added; butter and flour roux cooked 15-20 min to light brown; spice mix stirred in off the heat and set in the fridge. _(curl 200; JSON-LD recipe (ingredients and steps) read)_

## Own cooking judgment
- Homemade ground spice blend (SE, ATK brick, NYT brick); meat browned and onion cooked golden, carrots then stock (SE; ATK browns onion); potatoes and grated apple simmered until tender (SE; ATK potatoes and carrots about 20 min); butter-flour roux cooked to light brown with spices added off the heat (ATK, NYT, SE); roux stirred into the stew and simmered until thick (all); soy sauce (ATK). Beef chuck braised 1 h 15 min in place of chicken, the trimmed spice list and the reduced stock for salt are my own.
