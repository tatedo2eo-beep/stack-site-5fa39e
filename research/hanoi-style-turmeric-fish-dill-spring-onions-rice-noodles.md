## Own cooking judgment
- Firm white fish rubbed with a turmeric, ginger, shallot and garlic paste and marinated (NYT 12473 1 h; NYT 4782 2 h, refrigerated; NYT 6588 adds fish sauce to the rub); seared in hot oil until golden and cooked through (NYT 12473; NYT 6588); spring onions and dill cooked briefly in the oil and the fish returned about 1 min (NYT 12473; NYT 4782); served on rice noodles with mint, coriander, peanuts and a fish-sauce, lime, sugar, garlic and chilli dipping sauce (all three). My own: the fish is pan-seared rather than grilled, and quantities are scaled to keep salt under the site limit.
