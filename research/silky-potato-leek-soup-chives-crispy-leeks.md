## Own cooking judgment
- Leeks softened gently in butter without browning (NYT, SE, ATK)
- Potatoes, broth and bay simmered until tender (NYT, SE, ATK)
- Blended and finished with cream and chives (NYT, SE); crispy-leek garnish is this recipe's own
