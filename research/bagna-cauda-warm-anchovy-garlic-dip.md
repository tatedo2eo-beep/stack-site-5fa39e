## Own cooking judgment
- Garlic and anchovies melted into olive oil over the lowest heat, never coloured.
- Butter whisked in at the end for a golden, lightly emulsified dip.
- Served warm with raw fennel, pepper, celery and radicchio and boiled small potatoes.
