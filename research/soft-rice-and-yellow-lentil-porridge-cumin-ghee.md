## Own cooking judgment
- Rice and moong dal rinsed (NYT 1019202) and soaked (NYT 1022005); onion, ginger, cumin and turmeric cooked in ghee, then tomatoes (NYT 1022005); about 4 parts water to 1 part rice and dal for a soft result (NYT 1022005); ghee tempering with mustard seeds, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, curry leaves and chilli powder poured in (NYT 1019202); yoghurt and coriander to serve (NYT 1022005). Stovetop simmer for 25-30 min, adding the tempering at the end rather than the start, and the salt level are my own.
