## Own cooking judgment
- Masa harina with boiling water and oil rested 20 min, crack test and warm water 2 tsp at a time (ATK)
- Ball pressed into a cup, filled with cheese balls about 35 g, sealed and patted into a disc about 10 cm (ATK Pupusas; SE)
- Cooked in a lightly oiled cast-iron pan over medium heat about 4 min per side (SE); served with curtido and salsa (SE, NYT)
