## Sources actually opened

- [How to Dry Brine (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/how-to-dry-brine) [Top-tier: test kitchen / food science]: Salt all over including the cavity; uncovered rack in the fridge; dries the skin for crisping and seasons the meat. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Easy Roast Chicken (BBC Good Food)](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/easy-roast-chicken) [Top-tier: major food publication]: Oven 200 °C (180 °C fan); juices run clear; rest 15-20 min covered with foil before carving. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_

## Own cooking judgment
- 220 °C for 20 min then 190 °C (170 °C fan): a hotter start than BBC Good Food for browner skin, a lower finish so the breast does not overcook.
- Onion trivet and lemon/garlic/thyme cavity; simple pan juices.
- Both thigh and breast to 74 °C (USDA).
