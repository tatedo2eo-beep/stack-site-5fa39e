## Sources actually opened
- [Perfect Roast Chicken (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/perfect-roast-chicken-recipe-8384377) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Dry-brine the whole bird with salt, uncovered on a rack in the fridge; minimum 1 h, about 12 h recommended, up to 2 days. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [How to Dry Brine (Serious Eats)](https://www.seriouseats.com/how-to-dry-brine) [Tier 1 (locked list): Serious Eats]: Salt all over including the cavity; uncovered rack in the fridge; dries the skin for crisping and seasons the meat. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Salt-and-Pepper Roast Chicken (NYT Cooking)](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1017715-salt-and-pepper-roast-chicken) [Tier 2 (locked list): NYT Cooking]: Season ahead; roast at high heat (230 °C, or 200 °C if the oven smokes, adding time) until the skin is bronzed and crisp and the thigh reaches 74 °C; rest before carving. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Easy Roast Chicken (BBC Good Food)](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/easy-roast-chicken) [NOT on the locked list: context only, not relied on for method, ratios, temperatures or flavours]: Oven 200 °C (180 °C fan); juices run clear; rest 15-20 min covered with foil before carving. _(read via search extract; curl 200)_
- [Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (USDA FSIS)](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) [Safety database (locked list): USDA FSIS]: Ground meat 71.1 °C; all poultry incl. ground 73.9 °C; leftovers 73.9 °C. _(opened with WebFetch (curl gets 403 bot-block))_
- [USDA FoodData Central (API, SR Legacy)](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/) [Nutrition database (locked list): USDA FoodData Central]: Per-100 g nutrient values for every ingredient (ids in the nutrition note). _(queried via API)_
