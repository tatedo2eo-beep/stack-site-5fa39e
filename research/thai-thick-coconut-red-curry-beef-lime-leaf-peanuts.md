## Own cooking judgment
- Thin-sliced beef pre-simmered in water until fork-tender (ATK); coconut cream broken until oil separates before the paste goes in (NYT); paste fried until brick red 5-8 min (ATK); coconut milk, fish sauce and sugar, then beef simmered rapidly until reduced by half with oil streaks (ATK); lime leaves and chopped peanuts to finish (ATK). Coconut drizzle, chuck as an alternative cut and the panang paste quantity are my own.
