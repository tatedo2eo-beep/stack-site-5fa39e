## Own cooking judgment
- Turmeric-onion base and long simmer for the lamb; very finely chopped herbs fried about 20 min until dark and oily, with fenugreek (both); soaked and pierced dried limes pressed for sourness at the end (SE); saffron, rice and yoghurt (NYT). Tinned beans for reliability; plain rice instead of a crusted rice.
