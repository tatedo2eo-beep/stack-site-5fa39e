## Own cooking judgment
- Whole fish stuffed with lemon, garlic and herbs (all); coarse salt mixed with water and egg white to damp sand that holds when squeezed (ATK 13572; NYT 9190 egg white; NYT 1024955 water); fish set on salt beds and packed completely (NYT 1024955; ATK 13572); thermometer pushed through the crust behind the head (NYT 1024955); hot-oven bake and a 5 min rest (NYT 1024955; ATK 13572); crust cracked off and the salty top skin discarded before filleting (NYT 1024955; NYT 12727 skin removed). Bake time set to reach the site minimum of 63 °C.
